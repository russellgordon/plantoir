This site is the course's memory. Every class, concept, task, and
practice set lives here, and five habits make it fast to use.

## The five habits

1. **Search first.** Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac or
   <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and type any word.
   Search reads every page at once and is nearly always faster than
   navigating the sidebar.
2. **Hover before you click.** Rest your pointer on a link like
   [[Functions, Parameter Scope, and Pure Decomposition\|this one]] and a preview of the target page appears.
   You can confirm a definition without losing your place.
3. **Use the table of contents.** On longer pages, the right side
   shows the page's headings. Jump straight to what you need.
4. **Read the backlinks.** At the bottom of every page, *Backlinks*
   lists every page that links **to** this one. On a concept page that
   is an instant map of where the idea gets used.
5. **Predict before you unfold.** Practice pages fold their worked
   answers away. The fold is a tool, not a lock: commit to your own
   answer first, then open it. Peeking feels efficient and teaches
   nothing.
