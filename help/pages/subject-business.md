This page lists what a Business website made with Plantoir can show. Each item names a page of the ready-made Business course where you can see it, and the Help page that says how it is done.

## The ready-made Business course

One code has ready-made pages: **BOH4M**, Business Leadership: Management Fundamentals, Grade 12.

To get it, choose {{menu:newCourse}}, type BOH4M and leave {{control:newCourseSheetPrePopulateWithExampleContent}} turned on. [Make a new course](page:new-course) has the steps. It comes with its folders, its shared pages, and 86 class pages in four units for every section. The class pages are published and dated across the semester, so the first deploy may ask about classes dated later. See [Classes dated after the next class, at Deploy](page:later-classes).

To read it without teaching from it, put it on the Reference Courses shelf. See [Bring in last year's courses](page:import-for-reference).

Any other Business Studies code offers a starting point for the subject instead. Its folders are Concepts, Cases, Exercises, Projects, Portfolios, Tasks, Setup, Style, Tutorials and Curriculum.

## What the pages show

- **A case that prints, with notes folded under its questions.** The page The New Supervisor, in Cases, gives a case and then five questions. Under each question a note is folded away, saying what a strong answer includes. A student clicks a note to open it. The page has a {{control:printButton}} button on its date line. The class pages for Unit 1, Day 3 and Unit 1, Day 4 link to it. See [A handout that prints](page:printing), which also says what goes on the paper.

{{picture:subject-business-1}}
alt: The page The New Supervisor on the class website, with the Print button on its date line and a note folded under a question.

- **A question students check themselves, with its choices in a new order each time.** The page Levels of Management, in Concepts, has one under the heading Check the idea. It describes a day's work and asks what kind of work it is, with four choices. The choices are put in a new order each time the page loads. On paper they print in the order they were written. A student picks one and presses {{control:quizCheck}}, or presses {{control:quizShowAnswer}}. An explanation named Why appears after the student answers. Nothing a student answers reaches you. See [Questions students check themselves](page:quizzes).

{{picture:subject-business-2}}
alt: The question on the page Levels of Management, on the class website, with its four choices and the Check button.

- **Pages written once and linked from class pages.** The cases and the simulations are each written once. Class pages link to them. See [One lesson, written once](page:one-lesson).
- **A checklist students tick.** Every class page has one, under the heading Things to do before our next class. See [Checklists students tick](page:task-boxes).
- **Diagrams drawn from text.** The page Levels of Management, in Concepts, has one. See [Diagrams drawn from text](page:diagrams-from-text).
- **Tables, boxes and footnotes.** The page What This Site Can Do, in Style, shows each with examples from the course. See [Tables, boxes and footnotes](page:tables-boxes-footnotes).

{{assistant}}
- **Curriculum expectations and a map of them.** The Curriculum folder has a page for every expectation. Lesson pages show the expectations they address, under the heading Curriculum connection. See [Curriculum expectations and the coverage map](page:coverage).
{{/assistant}}

## Folders made for the subject

- **Cases** has ten pages. Four of them are The Hiring Decision, The Ethics Memo, The Flat Structure and The New Supervisor.
- **Simulations** has eight. Four of them are The In-Basket, The Negotiation, The Interview Panel and The Crisis Hour.
- **Style** holds How This Site Is Organised, What This Site Can Do and Writing About Management.
- **Tasks** is the folder whose work counts for marks.

[The folders Plantoir treats specially](page:special-folders) says what the folder for marks does.

## What BOH4M does not use

BOH4M shows no picture and no video, and has no success criteria for students to tick. A page can hold a picture or success criteria all the same. See [Pictures, PDFs and the Media folder](page:media) and [Let students track their progress](page:my-progress).
