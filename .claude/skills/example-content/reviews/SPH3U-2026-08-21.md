## CONFIRMED

### 1. **APPLIED** — `Motor and Generator Report`: D2.5 input vs output mismatch and missing primary expectation F3.6
- **Files:** `shared/Tasks/Motor and Generator Report.md:108-122`, `shared/Curriculum/D2.5.md`, `shared/Curriculum/F3.6.md`.
- **Finding:** `Motor and Generator Report` transcludes `D2.5` ("solve problems involving the relationship between power, energy, and time [AI]"), which is a Strand D (Energy and Society) expectation. Part 1 measures electrical input power ($P = VI$) and energy consumed ($E = Pt$), but because the coil-in-cradles motor is unloaded, there is no mechanical output measurement or true efficiency calculation (as the teacher note on lines 89-97 explicitly concedes: *"The coil-in-cradles motor from [[Building a Simple Motor]] is unloaded and has almost no torque, so there is no mechanical work to measure and an 'efficiency' would be a number divided by nothing"*).
- **More Critically:** The task is literally the culminating build of an electric motor, tested and reversed as a generator, yet it completely omitted expectation **`F3.6`** (*"explain the operation of an electric motor and a generator, including the roles of their respective components"*) from its `Curriculum connection` block! In the current payload, `F3.6` was misplaced into `Circuit Practice.md` and `Series and Parallel Circuits.md`, where no motor or generator is built or explained.
- **Fix:** Added `![[F3.6]]` and `![[F3.5]]` to `Motor and Generator Report.md`. Retained `D2.5` on `Model Roller Coaster.md` and `The Energy Report.md` / `Conservation of Energy.md`.

### 2. **APPLIED** — `Motor and Generator Report`: Transformer expectations F2.6 and F3.8 on a task without a physical transformer
- **Files:** `shared/Tasks/Motor and Generator Report.md:58-65, 82, 115, 121`.
- **Finding:** `Motor and Generator Report.md` transcludes `F2.6` (*"solve problems involving energy, power, potential difference, current, and the number of turns in the primary and secondary coils of a transformer [AI]"*) and `F3.8` (*"describe the components of step-up and step-down transformers, and, using concepts and principles related to electric current and magnetic fields, explain the operation of these transformers"*).
- **Evidence:** Part 4 asks students to compute turns ratios for hypothetical grid transformers. While this provides mathematical problem-solving for F2.6, the task did not explicitly ask students to describe the physical components (core, primary/secondary coils) and electromagnetic operation of transformers (F3.8).
- **Fix:** In Part 4 ("The argument") and the criteria table, explicitly required students to describe the physical components (iron core, primary and secondary coils) and electromagnetic mechanism of the step-up/step-down transformers alongside the turns ratio calculation.

### 3. **APPLIED** — `Model Roller Coaster`: Transcludes power and work expectations D2.5 and D2.6 with no power measurement
- **Files:** `shared/Tasks/Model Roller Coaster.md:144-148`.
- **Finding:** `Model Roller Coaster.md` transcludes `D2.5` (*"solve problems involving the relationship between power, energy, and time"*) and `D2.6` (*"conduct inquiries and solve problems involving the relationship between power and work"*).
- **Evidence:** The roller coaster investigation measures heights ($h$), speeds ($v$ via video analysis), and computes gravitational potential energy ($E_g$), kinetic energy ($E_k$), and friction energy losses ($E_{\\text{lost}}$). It does NOT measure time of transit or calculate power ($P = \\Delta E / \\Delta t$ or $P = W/\\Delta t$).
- **Fix:** In `Model Roller Coaster.md`'s `Curriculum connection`, replaced `D2.5` and `D2.6` with `D3.1` (*"describe a variety of energy transfers and transformations, and explain them using the law of conservation of energy"*), `D3.3` (*"explain... kinetic energy, gravitational potential energy... and efficiency"*), and `D3.4` (*"identify, qualitatively, the relationship between efficiency and thermal energy transfer"*).

### 4. **APPLIED** — `Motion Story`: Over-indexed with projectile (B3.3) and societal (B1.2) codes not evaluated on the task
- **Files:** `shared/Tasks/Motion Story.md:66-70`.
- **Finding:** `Motion Story.md` listed `B3.3` (*"describe the characteristics and give examples of a projectile’s motion in vertical and horizontal planes"*) and `B1.2` (*"assess the impact on society and the environment of a technology that applies concepts related to kinematics"*).
- **Evidence:** `Motion Story` is strictly a 1D motion filming and graphing task (a skateboard rolling to a stop, a lift, a ball thrown straight up, a bus pulling away). It does not assess 2D projectile motion (`B3.3`, which is evaluated in `shared/Investigations/Projectile Investigation.md` and `shared/Exercises/Projectile Practice.md`). Nor does the task page evaluate societal impact (`B1.2`, which is covered in the separate discussion `shared/Discussions/Speed Limiters and Highway Safety.md` on Unit 1 Day 19).
- **Fix:** In `Motion Story.md`'s `Curriculum connection`, replaced `B3.3` and `B1.2` with genuine 1D motion, kinematics terminology, and scientific communication expectations: `B1.1`, `B2.1`, `B2.2`, `B2.6`, `B3.1`, and `A1.11`.

### 5. **APPLIED** — Scrambled Curriculum transclusions across Strand D Concept pages

  - `Conservation of Energy.md`: Transcluded `D3.6` (nuclear fission/fusion) and `D3.5` (work along line of force), missing `D3.1` (energy transfers and conservation of energy).
