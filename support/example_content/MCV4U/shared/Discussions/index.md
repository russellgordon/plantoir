Some days the markers go down and the talking *is* the mathematics.
A conjecture only becomes knowledge after other people have pushed on
it — with questions, counterexamples, and better conjectures — and
the pushing is always aimed at ideas, never at the classmate brave
enough to voice one. In a course where the derivative rules are
conjectured before they are named and models get defended in public,
that habit is not a nicety; it is the method. Argue hard with the
claim; thank the person who made it.

Four conversations we return to all semester:

- [[When Will I Use This]] — the oldest question, and the course
  that finally gets to answer it without deferring
- [[Mistakes Are Data]] — the lost-inner-derivative museum; never
  erase
- [[Why Struggle Is the Point]] — stuck-and-thinking versus spinning
- [[What Makes a Model Good]] — fitting the points is not the job

Come with opinions. Expect to leave with different ones.
