Drop-in time outside scheduled class, for anyone — no appointment needed,
and no question too small to bring.

| When | Where | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesdays, lunch | This room | A quick question, a paragraph you want a second look at |
| Thursdays, right after school (until 4:00) | This room | Longer conversations — a whole draft, a task you're stuck on |
| Any checkpoint day, during class | Wherever we are | The five-minute version — see me before you leave |

If neither time works for you, say so and we'll find another one. The
schedule above is a starting point, not the only door.

See [[Getting Help]] for what to do if the problem is bigger than a single
question — a missed deadline, a text that's genuinely too much, anything
else this page doesn't cover.
