## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Predict the Circuit]] — margin round
2. [[The Specification]], day 4 — acceptance tests written: what someone
   else would do to decide whether you met each requirement
3. Peer build check: trade documents, and another bench runs one of
   your acceptance tests on paper and writes down what they could not
   do with your wording. Authors stay quiet while it happens — a
   question you answer out loud is one the document did not
4. Last fifteen minutes, together: [[Judging Your Own Work]] — I take a
   specification from an earlier year and judge it against the criteria
   table out loud, row by row, so the move is familiar before it is
   your own work being judged
