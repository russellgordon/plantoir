By the end of this course, you will be able to think computationally,
understand the hardware and software woven through your life, write
real programs in Python — and reason about what technology is doing to
the world, not just in it.

Put plainly, week to week that means:

- **Think before the machine** — break problems down, plan algorithms,
  and [[Computational Thinking|reason like a computer scientist]],
  often before touching a keyboard.
- **Build** — from [[Setting Up Python|first program]] to a
  [[Launch Day|working artifact for a real user]], with code others
  can read.
- **Understand the world's machinery** — the
  [[Hardware Inside the Box|hardware]],
  [[Networks and Connectivity|networks]], and
  [[Automation and Artificial Intelligence|AI systems]] everyday life
  now runs on.
- **Question it** — [[Who Owns Your Data|whose data]],
  [[Can a Machine Be Biased|whose bias]], and
  [[Whose Innovations Count|whose contributions]] — with evidence, not
  vibes.
- **Grow visibly** — your [[Dev Journal]] is where broken builds turn
  into evidence; see [[How Marks Work]].
