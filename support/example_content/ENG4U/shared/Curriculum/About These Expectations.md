The expectations in this folder are reproduced from **The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 11 and 12: English (revised 2007)**,
the course **ENG4U**, published by the Ontario Ministry of Education.

> [!info] The official source
> [English, Grade 12, University Preparation (ENG4U)](https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/english1112currb.pdf)
>
> © King's Printer for Ontario. Reproduced for educational use.

> [!warning] About the letters in the codes
> The 2007 document numbers expectations within each strand — 1.1, 2.3,
> and so on — but does not letter the strands themselves. The letters used
> here follow the order the strands appear in the document, which is how
> most Ontario English departments write them: **A** for Oral
> Communication, **B** for Reading and Literature Studies, **C** for
> Writing, and **D** for Media Studies. The numbers and the wording are the
> Ministry's; the letters are a local convention, and a course outline from
> another school may letter them differently.

Each page keeps the expectation's official **number** and every lesson,
discussion, and task in this course links back to the expectations it
addresses. The codes are also tags, so every page tagged `#C2` can be
gathered in one place.

## Why the codes matter more than they look

A code is a shared address. When a student asks "why are we reading this?",
the answer is a link. When a department writes a course outline, or a teacher
takes over a class partway through, the codes are what make one person's planning
legible to another.

> [!tip] For students
> You are not expected to memorise any of this. It is here so that the work
> can always be traced to a reason, and so that you can see the whole shape of
> the course in one place.
