> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class. **Groups of three.** **Participation is voluntary**
> — see the note below.

Measure how fast the body restores a variable after a deliberate
disturbance. Recovery rate is the measurement, not maximum heart rate.

> [!note] Voluntary, and private
> Anyone may take the recorder's role instead of the subject's, without
> giving a reason. Nobody's data is shared with the class attached to
> their name.

## Method

1. Resting heart rate: three counts, one minute each, seated.
2. A standardised exercise — the same step test, same duration, for every
   subject.
3. Heart rate immediately after, then every 30 seconds until it returns to
   within 10% of resting.
4. Record the recovery TIME as well as the curve.

## Analysis

1. Plot heart rate against time for at least three subjects.
2. Compare recovery times. What might explain differences, and which of
   your explanations could you actually test?
3. Explain the whole response as a feedback loop — stimulus, receptor,
   control centre, effector — using [[Homeostasis and Feedback]].
4. Which system delivered the fast response and which the slower one? See
   [[The Nervous System]] and [[The Endocrine System]].
