Grade 11 university English is where reading becomes an argument you can
defend in front of people who have read the same book. By January you
will be able to:

**Build a reading of a difficult text and defend it with evidence** —
including when somebody in the room has a better one.

**Write an essay that earns its conclusion**, rather than announcing it
in the introduction and repeating it at the end.

**Hear what a Canadian text is doing with the country it is written in**,
and say so precisely — which means naming who is speaking, from where,
and to whom.

**Lead a seminar** that other people want to be in.

**Read your own writing the way you read a text**: for structure, for
diction, for the sentence that is doing the work.
