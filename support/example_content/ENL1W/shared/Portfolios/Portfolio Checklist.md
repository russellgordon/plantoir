What has to be in your journal and folder before
[[The Portfolio Conversation]] in the last week. Tick the boxes as you go
(your browser keeps the ticks on this computer), or copy this into your
notebook.

## From the whole course

- [ ] [[Your First Entry]], from the first week, untouched
- [ ] At least twenty journal entries, dated
- [ ] Notes on your own reading — at least six books' worth of lines
- [ ] Your vocabulary page, per [[Where Words Come From]]

## The assessed pieces, with their drafts

- [ ] [[Who You Are, in Three Hundred Words]] — first draft AND final
- [ ] [[The Book Club]] — your reading log and your final response
- [ ] [[Say It Out Loud]] — your script, with your delivery marks on it
- [ ] [[The Fact Check]] — the claim, the check, and what you published
- [ ] [[The Argument]] — outline, draft with feedback, final

## The growth pair

- [ ] Two pieces chosen and paired, per [[Showing Growth]]
- [ ] Three named differences, written down
- [ ] One thing that has not changed yet, named honestly

## For the conversation itself

- [ ] The piece you are proudest of, and one sentence on why
- [ ] The piece that taught you the most, which is usually a different one
- [ ] One question you want to ask me about your own writing

> [!warning] The one people forget
> The drafts. A final copy with no draft behind it cannot show growth,
> and growth is what the last 30% of this course is about. Keep the messy
> pages — photograph them if paper goes missing in your life.
