Safe practice is not a mood. It is a set of specific things people do in
a specific order, every period, whether or not anyone is watching.

## Isolating a machine, and its guards

Lockout means the energy is switched off, physically disconnected, and
cannot be restored while somebody is working on the machine — because a
machine that starts with a hand inside it does not stop. Full lockout on
fixed equipment is trained staff's job; your version is the same
principle smaller. **Unplug it and keep the plug in your own sight**
before you change a blade or clear a jam.

Guards stay on — off only for the person authorised to service that
machine, and back before it runs. Find one missing, damaged, or tied back
and the machine is out of service: do not run it, tag it, report it.

## Housekeeping, loads, and water

Most site injuries are the floor's fault. Cords routed around walkways,
offcuts cleared as they are made, spills cleaned when they happen,
wheelbarrows out of the traffic line, tools back on the marked wall.
Wash your hands before eating.

Plan the route before you lift and check the far end is clear. Wheel it
if you can. When you do lift: load close to your body, feet apart, knees
bent, no twisting, and set it down before you rearrange your grip.
Anything awkward, heavy, or above waist height is a two-person job.

At the shoreline nobody works alone and nobody enters the water. Stage
tools back from the bank and work facing it — [[Planting a Shoreline]].

## When something goes wrong

Stop, and stop the people around you; power off if you can do it safely.
Do not become a second casualty — get help rather than going in after
someone. Then tell your teacher and report it: injury, near miss, spill,
fire, damage, or a rule you have just realised you broke.

Copy this into your work record. You can tick the boxes below here too;
your browser keeps the ticks on this computer, and nobody else sees them.

- [ ] Machine isolated before hands go near it
- [ ] Guards present, floors and walkways clear
- [ ] Route planned, help asked for, load kept close, everything reported
