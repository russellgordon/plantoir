By the end of this course, you will be able to find a story, report it
honestly, produce it across media — words, images, sound, video,
layout — and publish it where your community actually reads; and you
will have taken the same design process into the shop, to make
something a real person uses. In the curriculum's own words, the
through-lines are:

Put plainly, week to week that means:

- **Find and tell true stories** — [[News Values|news judgement]],
  [[Interviewing|interviews]] that get past yes-and-no, and
  [[The Inverted Pyramid|writing]] a reader cannot put down.
- **Make media that carries them** — [[Making Photographs|photographs]]
  with intent, [[Shooting Video|video]] that cuts together,
  [[Clean Audio|audio]] worth listening to, and
  [[Design Principles|design]] that respects the reader.
- **Publish like professionals** — the
  [[Audiences and Platforms|right channel for the right audience]],
  under [[Our Newsroom Standards]]: accurate, fair, consented,
  credited.
- **Make things that work** — [[Laser Cutting|cut]] and
  [[3D Printing|printed]] parts, designed for a real user, measured,
  tested, and made better, safely.
- **Work as a team on deadline** — because a publication is a promise
  a whole newsroom makes together.
- **Grow visibly** — your [[Newsroom Journal]] is where spiked stories
  turn into evidence you can point at, and
  [[Judging Your Own Work]] is how you use it before a deadline rather
  than after one.
