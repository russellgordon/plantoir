Staring at an empty studio floor with forty minutes to make a phrase is a
horrible feeling, and it is entirely avoidable. You already know twenty or
thirty movements by heart — the ones you have done at the start of every
class since the first week. They are the shortest route from exercise to
composition, because you can stop thinking about how to do them and spend
all of your attention on choices instead.

The method is one sentence: **take a warm-up exercise and change two
elements of dance about it.** One change usually produces a variation. Two
changes usually produce something that no longer looks like an exercise at
all.

## Four worked examples

| Exercise | Change these two | What comes out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spinal roll down | Four times faster, sharp | A fall, not an exercise |
| Plié in second | Travel it, add a spiral | A sinking motif that crosses |
| Arm circles | Do it with the hip, low | An isolation phrase, grounded |
| Counted walk | Canon in four, half speed | An entrance that fills a stage |

Try one of these for real before you read on — the page cannot make the
point, only the floor can.

## Why it works, and where it stops

It works because the material is already in your muscles, so nothing is
lost to remembering. It also works because warm-up exercises are *clean*:
they were designed to be readable, symmetrical, and repeatable, which are
three of the things a phrase needs anyway.

Where it stops is the ending. A warm-up exercise repeats until the teacher
says stop, so it has no ending built into it — and a phrase with no ending
is still an exercise, no matter how much you changed. Decide where yours
arrives and what the last shape is. That is the difference between material
and choreography, and it is what [[Phrase Building]] is about.

Everything here depends on knowing the five elements well enough to pick two
on purpose, so keep [[The Elements of Dance]] beside you the first few
times. When it comes to [[The Phrase]], nobody will ask where your material
came from — a rehearsed roll-down turned inside out is as legitimate a
source as anything else.
