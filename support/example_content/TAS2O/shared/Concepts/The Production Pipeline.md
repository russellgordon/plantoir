Somewhere during [[The Athletics Package]] you learned this the
expensive way: the battery that died in the third quarter, the
interview nobody booked, the shot the edit needed that nobody had
gathered. Every one of those disasters happened days before it
happened — in planning that did not happen. Production is one arc with
three phases, and each phase can only work with what the previous one
provided.

## One arc, three phases

```mermaid
graph LR
    A["Pre-production — decide"] --> B["Production — gather"]
    B --> C["Post-production — shape"]
    C --> D["Publish"]
```

**Pre-production** is every decision made before recording: the story
proposal, the shot list, the interview questions, the gym booked, the
batteries charged. **Production** is the gathering — shooting,
recording, interviewing — and it is the phase you cannot repeat: the
championship happens once. **Post-production** is the shaping — the
edit, the mix, the captions, the final checks — and it inherits
everything: the edit can only arrange what production captured, and
production can only capture what pre-production arranged.

## Planning is cheaper than reshooting

The same change costs wildly different amounts in each phase. Adding a
shot in pre-production is one line on the shot list; during production
it is a scramble; in post-production it is often impossible, because
the moment is gone. This is why professionals seem to spend forever
planning: a mistake found in a storyboard costs a pencil stroke, and
the same mistake found in the edit costs the story.

For a news team, the pipeline is project management: plan the arc
backwards from [[Publication Day]], so every phase has a deadline and
the edit is never the thing that slips. Track where each story sits in
your [[Newsroom Journal]], hand work between phases the way
[[Filing a Story]] describes, and treat [[Backing Up Your Media]] as
the pipeline's insurance policy — footage that exists in one place is
one dropped card away from a reshoot no schedule can absorb. The
day-to-day rhythm that keeps all this moving is described in
[[How the Newsroom Runs]].
