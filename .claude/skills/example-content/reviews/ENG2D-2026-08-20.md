## The single most important finding

**Finding 2**, narrowly ahead of finding 1. Finding 1 is bigger in principle —
eight pages of Ministry text, one of them the payload's headline quotation,
stopping mid-sentence — but it is a known family defect with a known cure and
ENG3U proves the source is recoverable.

Finding 2 is the one a teacher hits. ENG2D is a semester course that runs
8 September to 20 January, and it tells its students on six pages that the
year ends in June, that the literacy-test periods are in the spring, that the
media unit is in April, and that they will pair a September paragraph with a
May one. `Unit 1, Day 2` sends them to `Learning Goals` with the words "what
you will be able to do in **January**", and `Learning Goals` opens "By
**June**". A teacher who adopts this payload publishes both of those sentences
on the same afternoon, and the first student to notice will be right.

## MY OWN ADVERSARIAL PASS — what the fix round broke, found before reporting

Run against my own diff, with the same rules: flatten whitespace before
searching, loop over files rather than piping through `xargs`, and verify
against the file rather than against what I remembered writing. Six real
defects, five of them mine.

1. **I sent students to a task that had not launched.** My first version of
