## Agenda

1. Meet [[Hamlet]] — what a revenge tragedy promises its audience, and why
   Shakespeare wrote almost the whole thing in verse
2. Diagnostic cold-read: an unseen passage of Shakespearean-style verse,
   three questions written in class — what's happening, what's hard about
   the language, and one guess at what an unfamiliar word might mean. Kept,
   not marked, just so I know where this unit is starting from.
3. Look ahead: after tomorrow's verse workshop, Act 1 begins as a staged
   reading, with roles handed out
