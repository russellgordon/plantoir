This is the site itself, not the course — a two-minute guide to moving
around it before you need to move around it under pressure.

## Getting to today's class

Your section's landing page always shows the most recent published class
under "Most Recent Class." From there, the class page's agenda links out to
everything that day uses.

## Finding an old class

Open **All Classes**, in the sidebar under your section — every class page
you've had, newest at the top. If you remember roughly when something
happened ("that was around the start of Unit 3"), scroll to about the right
place.

## Searching for a term

The search icon at the top of the sidebar searches every page's title and
text at once — faster than browsing if you already know a word or a title.

## Following a link

Blue text you can click is a link to another page — [[How This Site Is Organised]]
is one, if you want to try it. A page sometimes also shows
another page's content inline, without you needing to click at all; that is
called a transclusion, and you'll see it most on task pages that pull in
their curriculum connections or on your section's landing page, which
transcludes the latest class directly.

## Seeing what links to a page

Most pages show a **backlinks** list — every other page that links to the
one you're on. If you land somewhere unfamiliar and want to know how it
connects to the course, scroll down and check.

## What you can't do here

You cannot edit anything on this site, and no checklist, form, or button
saves anything you click. This is a published record of the course, not a
workspace — your actual writing happens somewhere else (a document, a
notebook, whatever this course tells you to hand work in through).

Ready to try it for real? [[Scavenger Hunt]] is a short set of find-it
challenges that will have you using everything above by the end.
