Not much, and all of it matters.

| Bring | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A notebook you keep all term | Cases are worked with a pen; your notes become exam preparation |
| A device you can write and research on | Most work is written and researched; the school has loaners |
| Your file of case notes | Every case we work gets re-used, especially in review |
| Something to read business news on | The weekly headline habit — see [[Reading the Business Press]] |

## For the days that are different

- **Presentation days.** Dress the way you would to meet the client. It
  is not a mark, and it changes how a room listens to you.
- **Client days in Unit 4.** Notebook, questions prepared, and arrive
  early. You are a guest in someone's organization.
- **The case examination.** Pens, a watch you can see, and nothing else —
  it is a closed-book paper with the case supplied.

## Software

Everything can be done with a word processor, a spreadsheet, and slide
software, all of which the school provides. If you prefer something else,
that is fine, as long as what you hand in opens.

> [!tip] Keep everything in one folder, backed up
> You will re-use your own work: the profile from the first week appears again
> at the end of the course, and your case notes are the best exam preparation there is. A
> lost file at the end of the course is a real problem — see
> [[The Tools of the Trade]].
