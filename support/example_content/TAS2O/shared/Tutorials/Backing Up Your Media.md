A memory card is a loan, not a home. The card in your camera or
recorder holds the only copy of work that cannot be re-shot — the
save, the quote, the light that afternoon — and cards get formatted,
fill up mid-shoot, and ride home in pockets. Every working newsroom
treats media as unpublished until it is backed up, and so do we.

## Ingest, name, duplicate — before anything else

The moment a shoot ends, before any editing, before even looking at
what you got:

1. **Ingest.** Copy everything off the card to the team drive — all
   of it, including the frames you already suspect are misses. The
   contact sheet needs the misses; so does your
   [[Newsroom Journal]].
2. **Name.** The copy lands in a folder named date-plus-slug —
   `2026-03-06-semifinal` — matching the story it belongs to, per
   [[Filing a Story]].
3. **Duplicate.** A second copy in a second location. Only then does
   the card go back in the camera, and only then may it be cleared —
   the order [[Our Newsroom Standards]] sets for equipment returns.

## Folder discipline on a team drive

One folder per story; inside it, `raw`, `selects`, and `exports`.
Raw is never edited and never renamed — it is the newsroom's
negative. Selects are copies you chose and may crop and cut;
exports are what gets filed. Anyone on your desk should be able to
find your media without you, because on publication day they will
need to.

## The two-places rule

Media exists in two places that do not travel in the same bag —
team drive and a second copy, not card and the same card. Ask "if
this one thing were lost or wiped tonight, does the story survive?"
If the answer is no, you are not done ingesting. The card never
counts as one of the two places.
