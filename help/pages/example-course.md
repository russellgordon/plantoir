Plantoir comes with a finished example course that you can add to your working folder, change as you like, and remove. Its subject is Grade 9 science, and it has two sections.

## Add the example course

1. Choose {{menu:newCourse}} to open the {{control:newCourseOrClub}} sheet.
2. At the top of the sheet, under {{control:newToThis}}, press {{control:addExampleCourse}}. The sheet shows its progress while the example is copied into your working folder.

{{picture:example-course-1}}
alt: The top of the New Course or Club sheet, where the New to this? box holds the Add Example Course button.

3. When it has finished, press {{control:wizardClose}}. The example is now in the sidebar, and its Section 1 is selected.
4. Press {{control:previewButton}}. Section 1's website shows in the window.

The first preview on a {{machine}} takes longer than later ones. [The first preview is slow](page:slow-first-build) says why.

## Try things on it

The example is an ordinary course. Every menu works on it as it does on a course of your own.

- [Tour the example course](page:tour-example-course) is a guided look at how it is put together.
- Its classes carry fixed dates, from September 2026 to January 2027. Adding the example does not move them to this year.
- Before you deploy one of its sections, choose where the example deploys. Open its course settings and look under {{control:deployingPart}}. [Choose where a section deploys](page:destinations) explains the choices.
- You can add the example more than once. A second copy is given a different code, so the two sit side by side in the sidebar.

## Remove it

When you no longer need the example, select it in the sidebar and use {{menu:removeCourse}} to remove it. [Rename or remove a course](page:rename-remove) has the steps.

A removed course is kept as an archive, so it can be brought back. See [Bring back a removed course](page:archive).

## If it does not work

- **{{menu:newCourse}} is greyed.** No working folder is open, or the folder is still being made ready. Open a working folder, or wait a moment and look again.
- **{{control:addExampleCourse}} is greyed.** The working folder is still being made ready. Rest the pointer on the button to read the reason.
