Written in the first week, kept, and read again at the end.

## Write about all four

1. **The last thing you read that you chose yourself.** What was it, and
   what did you make of it?
2. **What you can already do as a writer**, specifically. "I can build an
   argument" is more useful than "I am good at essays".
3. **What you find hardest** — starting, structure, quoting, speaking in
   discussion, finishing.
4. **What you want to be able to do by January.** In your own words.

A page, handwritten, twenty minutes.

## Why

Because in January you will read it beside what you can then do, and
write about the difference — see [[Showing Growth]]. Most students cannot
feel their own progress; this is the evidence that it happened.

> [!note] Who reads it
> Nobody, unless you decide otherwise. I check it exists.
