What you should be able to do by the end of AVI1O, in plain words.

## By June you should be able to

- **Generate ideas** and develop them — not wait for one.
- **Make work in several media**, choosing the medium for a reason.
- **Use the elements and principles deliberately**, and name what you
  used.
- **Look at a work properly**: describe it before judging it, and
  support a reading with what is visibly there.
- **Say what art is for** in a society, and what it has done to how you
  see.
- **Prepare and present work** so that it reaches an audience.
- **Work safely and responsibly** with materials, and credit other
  people's images.

## The self-check that matters

Two things. Can you take an idea from a blank page to a finished piece
that says what you meant? And can you stand in front of an unfamiliar
work and say something true about it that a viewer would not have
noticed alone? That is the course, and it is what
[[The Portfolio and Reflection]] asks.
