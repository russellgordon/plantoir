A sight passage, read closely. Read it twice, then answer. Your score is
at the bottom of the page.

> He counted the tins twice. Then he moved four of them to the back of
> the cupboard, where the light did not reach, and closed the door
> quietly, the way you close a door when somebody is asleep.

> [!quiz] Which details show that he does not want the tins found?
> - [x] He moves them "to the back of the cupboard"
> - [x] He puts them "where the light did not reach"
> - [ ] He locks the cupboard
> - [ ] He hides all of the tins
>
> > [!explain]- Why
> > Hiding is about *where*: the back of the cupboard, out of the light.
> > He closes the door but never locks it, and he moves four of the tins,
> > not all of them.

> [!quiz] What does the last comparison, "the way you close a door when somebody is asleep", suggest?
> - [ ] He is tired and wants to go to bed
> - [x] There is someone in the house he does not want to wake, or to know
> - [ ] The cupboard door is broken
> - [ ] He is afraid of the dark
>
> > [!explain]- Why
> > You close a door that way for somebody else. The care is for a person
> > nearby, the same person he may be hiding the tins from.

> [!quiz] Which word in the passage tells you how he closes the door?
> > [!answer]-
> > quietly
