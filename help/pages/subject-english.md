This page lists what an English website made with Plantoir can show. Each item names a page of a ready-made English course where you can see it, and the Help page that says how it is done.

## The ready-made English courses

Four codes have ready-made pages:

- **ENL1W**, English, Grade 9
- **ENG2D**, English, Grade 10
- **ENG3U**, English, Grade 11
- **ENG4U**, English, Grade 12

To get one, choose {{menu:newCourse}}, type its code and leave {{control:newCourseSheetPrePopulateWithExampleContent}} turned on. [Make a new course](page:new-course) has the steps. Each comes with its folders, its shared pages, and 86 class pages in four units for every section. The class pages are published and dated across the semester, so the first deploy may ask about classes dated later. See [Classes dated after the next class, at Deploy](page:later-classes).

To read one without teaching from it, put it on the Reference Courses shelf. See [Bring in last year's courses](page:import-for-reference).

Any other English code offers a starting point for the subject instead. Its folders are Concepts, Readings, Discussions, Writing, Portfolios, Tasks, Setup, Style, Tutorials and Curriculum.

## What the pages show

Two of the examples below are in ENG2D only: the question students check themselves, and the handout that prints.

- **A work studied, written once.** Each work a class studies is one page in the Reading folder, and class pages link to it. ENG2D's page Romeo and Juliet is linked from three class pages: Unit 1, Day 18, Unit 2, Day 11 and Unit 2, Day 17. See [One lesson, written once](page:one-lesson).
- **A question students check themselves.** ENG2D's page Romeo and Juliet has one, under the heading One line to check before we get there. It asks what Juliet means by a line in Act 2, and gives four choices. A student picks one and presses {{control:quizCheck}}, or presses {{control:quizShowAnswer}}. An explanation named Why appears after the student answers. Nothing a student answers reaches you. See [Questions students check themselves](page:quizzes).

{{picture:subject-english-1}}

- **A handout that prints, with its answers folded.** ENG2D's page The Prologue, Line by Line, in Exercises, sets out the fourteen lines of the Prologue in a numbered table. Seven questions follow. On the website each answer is folded under its question, and a student clicks to open it. The page has a {{control:printButton}} button on its date line. The class page for Unit 2, Day 1 links to it. See [A handout that prints](page:printing), which also says what goes on the paper.

- **A checklist students tick.** Every class page in all four has one, under the heading Things to do before our next class. See [Checklists students tick](page:task-boxes).
- **Diagrams drawn from text.** ENG2D's page Plot and Structure, in Concepts, has one. So does ENL1W's page The Argument, in Tasks. See [Diagrams drawn from text](page:diagrams-from-text).
- **What a page can hold.** Each of the four has a page named What This Site Can Do, in its Style folder. It shows what a page can hold, with examples of its own.

{{assistant}}
- **Curriculum expectations and a map of them.** Each of the four has a Curriculum folder with a page for every expectation. Its lesson pages show the expectations they address, under the heading Curriculum connection. See [Curriculum expectations and the coverage map](page:coverage).
{{/assistant}}

## Folders made for the subject

- **Reading** holds one page for each work studied. ENG2D's are Media Texts We Study, Moon of the Crusted Snow, Poems for Grade 10, Romeo and Juliet and Short Stories.
- **Exercises** is in all four.
- **Style** holds How This Site Is Organised, What This Site Can Do and Writing About Literature. In ENL1W the last is Writing About Texts.
- **Tasks** is the folder whose work counts for marks.

[The folders Plantoir treats specially](page:special-folders) says what the folder for marks does.

## What these four do not use

None of the four shows a picture or a video, and none has success criteria for students to tick. A page can hold each all the same. See [Pictures, PDFs and the Media folder](page:media) and [Let students track their progress](page:my-progress).
