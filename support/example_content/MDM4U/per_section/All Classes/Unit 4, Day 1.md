## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Graph Talks]] — a chart from this week's news with
   the headline covered up. Write the headline you think it deserves
2. Random groups: one published study, four questions — who was
   asked, how many, compared with what, and who paid for it. My
   diagnostic for the unit: whichever of the four this room reaches
   last is the one Days 3 and 4 spend the most time on
3. Consolidate: the reading checklist, assembled out of your four
   questions rather than handed to you finished
4. Discuss: [[When Will I Use This]] — actuaries, epidemiologists,
   pollsters, and community planners run that checklist for a living
5. Practise: [[Regression and Inference Practice]], second set
