These questions follow [[Transformations of Functions]] — the recipe
$y = a\,f(k(x - d)) + c$ applied to the parent functions. Sketch on
paper first; [[Using Desmos]] is for *checking*, after you have
committed to an answer.

## Describe the moves

1. Describe how the graph of $y = f(x - 3) + 2$ relates to the graph
   of $y = f(x)$.
2. Describe the transformations in $y = -2f(x)$.
3. Describe the transformations in $y = f(2x) - 5$.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Slide right 3, slide up 2. The bracket lies about direction —
> $x - 3$ moves *right* — and the outside term tells the truth,
> as always.

> [!success]- Answer 2
> Vertical stretch by a factor of 2, plus a reflection in the
> $x$-axis. Both act on outputs: every $y$-value is doubled and
> flipped.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Horizontal compression by a factor of $\frac{1}{2}$ — inside moves
> run backwards, so multiplying $x$ by 2 *squeezes* — then a slide
> down 5.

## Analyse and sketch

4. Starting from $y = \sqrt{x}$, describe the transformations that
   produce $y = \sqrt{x + 1} - 4$, then state its domain and range.
5. For $y = -(x - 2)^2 + 3$: vertex, opening direction, domain, and
   range.
6. For $y = \dfrac{1}{x - 3} + 2$: describe the transformations and
   state the equations of both asymptotes.

> [!success]- Answer 4
> Left 1, down 4. The domain slides with the graph: $x \ge -1$. The
> range slides too: $y \ge -4$. Sketch check: the "corner" of the
> root curve now sits at $(-1, -4)$.

> [!success]- Answer 5
> Vertex $(2, 3)$, opening down (the negative $a$). Domain: all real
> numbers; range: $y \le 3$ — the vertex is now a *maximum*, so the
> range hangs below it.

> [!success]- Answer 6
> The parent $\frac{1}{x}$ slid right 3 and up 2. Its asymptotes
> travel with it: vertical $x = 3$, horizontal $y = 2$. (Hence
> domain $x \ne 3$ and range $y \ne 2$ — asymptotes and restrictions
> are the same facts in different costumes.)

## Points and backwards questions

7. The point $(4, 2)$ lies on $y = f(x)$. Find its image on
   (a) $y = 3f(x - 1) + 2$ and (b) $y = f(2x)$.
8. Write the equation of the function obtained when $y = \sqrt{x}$ is
   stretched vertically by a factor of 2, reflected in the $x$-axis,
   then translated right 5 and up 3.

> [!success]- Answer 7
> (a) Outputs: $y = 3(2) + 2 = 8$; inputs: $x = 4 + 1 = 5$. Image:
> $(5, 8)$. (b) Inside moves run backwards, so the $x$-coordinate
> *divides* by 2: image $(2, 2)$.

> [!success]- Answer 8
> $y = -2\sqrt{x - 5} + 3$. The order given — stretch and reflect
> first, slide second — is exactly the order the recipe applies, so
> each instruction maps to one letter: $a = -2$, $d = 5$, $c = 3$.
> Check one point: the parent's $(4, 2)$ should land at
> $(9, -1)$, and substituting $x = 9$ gives $-2(2) + 3 = -1$. ✓
