Everything this course does points at one or more of these expectations,
from **CHA3U — American History, Grade 11, University Preparation**.
Every source, investigation, and task links back to the codes it
addresses.

> [!info] Where these come from
> The wording below is the Ontario curriculum's own, from the 2015
> Canadian and world studies document, which has not been revised for
> this course. See [[About These Expectations]]. An Ontario committee
> decided which parts of the American past a student here studies — a
> fact worth keeping in view while you study them.

Strand A is not a unit. Its own stem says *throughout this course*, and
the document asks that it be woven through the other four rather than
taught in a block at the start. The four period strands each ask the
same three questions of a different era: what the social, economic, and
political context was, how groups came into conflict and cooperation,
and what the period did to identity, citizenship, and heritage.
