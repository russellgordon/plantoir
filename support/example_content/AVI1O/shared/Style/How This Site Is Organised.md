This site is the course: the schedule, the ideas, the studio practice, and
the expectations behind all of it.

| Folder | Holds | You will use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **All Classes** | One page per period — the agenda and what to do next | Every day |
| **Concepts** | The ideas, stated cleanly | After the class that met one |
| **Techniques** | How each medium actually behaves in your hands | Before and during a media block |
| **Studio Time** | The working periods, and what each is for | Whenever you are making |
| **Critiques** | Looking at work — others' and your own | Before every critique |
| **Portfolios** | The sketchbook, the portfolio, and how work gets recorded | All semester |
| **Tasks** | Assessed work, with the criteria | Whenever something is due |
| **Tutorials** | Using the site, photographing work, writing about art | The first time you need one |
| **Setup** | How the studio runs, including materials and safety | Week one |
| **Style** | How the site works | Now |

## How the pieces connect

Class pages are the spine. Each one names the looking, the work, and the
clearing up, and links to whatever that period actually consists of. The
backlinks at the foot of a page show which classes used it, so
[[Materials and Safety]] carries its own list of every period where it
mattered.

Content pages end with a **Curriculum connection** — the Ministry
expectations that page addresses, in the Ministry's own words. The site
builds a coverage map from those links, so you can see what the course has
actually taught rather than what it intended to.

## Two conventions

- **Concept pages come after the making.** [[The Elements of Design]]
  assumes your hands have already met the idea, which is why it reads like
  a set of names for things you noticed rather than a list to memorise.
- **The newest class is on your section's landing page.** If you were
  away, start there and work backwards.

[[What This Site Can Do]] is the tour of the features, and
[[Using This Site]] is the five-minute version for week one.
