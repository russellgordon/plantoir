## Agenda

1. Welcome — the rhythm we will live by: [[How Math Class Works]] and
   [[Our Classroom Norms]]
2. Number talk: [[Estimation Duels]] — how many text messages did this
   room send yesterday? The closest *reasoned* estimate wins
3. Random groups: [[The Birthday Problem]] at the boards — how many
   people must be in a room before two of them probably share a
   birthday?
4. Discuss: nearly every group guessed high. What was your first guess
   counting, and what should it have been counting instead? This one
   is a diagnostic and not a test — I am finding out how this room
   reasons about chance before anything has been taught, and what I
   hear today decides how long we spend on counting before probability
5. The year ahead: [[Learning Goals]] — where this course is going
6. A quick tour with [[Using This Site]] — every page from today is
   already linked above
