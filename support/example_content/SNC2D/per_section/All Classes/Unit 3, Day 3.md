## Agenda

1. Warm-up: name the components of the climate system, from memory
2. The system, drawn: sun, atmosphere, oceans, land, ice, and living
   things — and the arrows between them
3. Where energy enters and leaves, and what happens in between
4. Trace one ocean current across your drawing: where it takes heat up,
   where it puts it down, and which coastline gets its weather because of it
