## Power is a rate

$$P = \frac{W}{\Delta t} = \frac{E}{\Delta t}$$

measured in watts. One watt is one joule per second. Two students who
climb the same stairs do the same WORK; the faster one develops more
POWER. A 100 W bulb is not brighter because it holds more energy — it
converts more each second.

## Efficiency is a fraction

$$\text{efficiency} = \frac{E_{useful\ out}}{E_{in}} \times 100\%$$

No real system reaches 100%. Not because engineering is bad, but because
some energy always leaves as heat — the one form that will not fully come
back. An incandescent bulb is about 5% efficient as a light and 95%
efficient as a heater.

| Device | Roughly |
| --- | --- |
| Electric kettle | 90% |
| Electric motor | 85% |
| Car engine (petrol) | 25% |
| Incandescent bulb (as light) | 5% |

## Why this is a "Society" strand

Every efficiency number above is also an argument about money, emissions,
and who pays for them. That is what [[Where Our Electricity Comes From]]
is about, and why the Ministry files it under Energy and Society rather
than under Energy alone.
