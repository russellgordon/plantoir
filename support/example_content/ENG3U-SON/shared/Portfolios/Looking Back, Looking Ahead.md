A short, honest check-in at the halfway point of the course — not a
report card, and not marked, just a chance to look at where you started and
set something for where you're headed.

## Three prompts

Written in class, in one sitting, near the end of Unit 2:

1. **What's actually gotten easier?** Not what you're supposed to say got
   easier — something you can point to. Maybe building a thesis used to
   take you the whole period and now it takes fifteen minutes.
2. **What still feels shaky?** Naming this honestly is the useful part. If
   nothing feels shaky, look harder — everyone has something.
3. **One thing to try differently in Unit 3.** Specific enough that you
   could check, at the end of Unit 3, whether you actually did it.

## Why this sits here and not in a task

This page is assessment *as* learning — it's for you, not for a mark. It
practices the same judgement [[Judging Your Own Work]] does, aimed at your
growth across the course instead of at one piece of writing.

> [!tip] Keep what you wrote
> Whatever you write here is worth reading again in January, once Unit 4's
> Independent Culminating Study asks you to reflect one more time on how
> far you've come.
