What you should be able to do by the end of the course, in plain words first and
in the Ministry's words underneath.

Career Studies is a half credit. It runs from the first week to the first week
of the course, and then Civics takes over this period. Nine weeks is short
enough that nothing here is filler, and short enough that a question left
unasked for a fortnight has cost you a fifth of the course.

## By the end of the course you should be able to

- **Name what you can already do**, and back every claim with a situation
  somebody else could confirm.
- **Decide on purpose**, using a strategy you can name, and say
  afterwards how well it actually served you.
- **Read the work around you** — what is changing in a field, and what
  that means for the skills you are building now.
- **Apply for something properly**: a posting read closely, a résumé that
  answers it, and a message that still sounds like you.
- **Cost a year honestly**, every figure sourced and dated.
- **Hold a plan and change it**, and say what changed it.
- **Look after yourself while you work**, and know who to go to before it
  is urgent.

## The self-check that matters

Hand your plan to an adult who does not already know your reasoning. Can
they see where you are going, what it costs, what would stop you, and
what you would do then? That is the whole course, and it is exactly what
[[The Plan Defence]] asks of you.
