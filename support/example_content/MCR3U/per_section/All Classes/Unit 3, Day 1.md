## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Estimation Duels]] — how tall is the gym ceiling?
2. Random groups — [[How High Is That|How High Is That?]] — out of the classroom, and
   measure something unreachable. Unmarked, and deliberately so: I want to
   see which groups reach for a ratio and which reach for a guess, because
   that is what Grade 10 trigonometry left standing
3. Back at the boards: fold a square, halve an equilateral triangle —
   exact ratios with no calculator anywhere
4. Consolidate: [[Special Angles]] — five angles you now own outright
5. Check your understanding — exact values only, no decimals allowed
