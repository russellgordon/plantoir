Times I am in the room and free to work with you. No appointment, no
reason needed.

| When | Where | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday, lunch | Room 118 | Whatever is due this week |
| Wednesday, after school until 4:00 | Room 118 | Research, applications, and the budget spreadsheet |
| Any morning before period 1, by arrangement | Room 118 | Quick questions, marks, deadlines |

This course is nine weeks long, so these sessions are worth more here
than in a full-year course. A question left until the following week has
cost you a ninth of the time available.

> [!tip] Bring the thing itself
> The posting, the spreadsheet, the draft on a screen. Ten minutes with
> the document open beats half an hour of describing it.

In the second-last week the Wednesday session is for defence rehearsals,
and in the last week it is for the defences that do not fit the
scheduled block. Book it either way, so you are not sharing the room
with four other people.

If none of these times work, say so and we will find one. See
[[Getting Help]] for the other ways to reach me.
