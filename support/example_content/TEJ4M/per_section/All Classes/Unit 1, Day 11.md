## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Name That Part]] — the derating round. Name the part,
   then name the figure that decides whether it survives a hot attic
2. Bench: run a quarter-watt resistor at four-fifths of its rating and
   read its body temperature with the infrared thermometer. Predict
   the rise first; keep fingertips away, because the answer burns
3. Name it: [[Reliability and Derating]] — margin as a decision you
   record and defend, not a feeling you have
4. [[The Specification]], day 2 — the one-sentence job, the block
   diagram, and the power budget worked out block by block from parts
   you have chosen, to the standard in
   [[Writing Documentation Somebody Can Build From]]
5. Practise what the supply taught you:
   [[Power and Regulation Practice]]
