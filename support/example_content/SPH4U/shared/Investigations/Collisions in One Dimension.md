> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class, written up in the next period and handed in at the
> bell — the second of the marked [[Investigation Write-Ups]]. **Groups of
> three at the track, write-up yours alone.** Bring the energy question
> with you: is kinetic energy conserved here?

Two carts on a track, magnets for elastic collisions and velcro for
inelastic ones, and photogates or video for the speeds.

## Safety

Carts leave the track if the collision is violent. End stops on, and
speeds kept modest.

## Method

Run at least four collisions:

1. Elastic, equal masses, one at rest.
2. Elastic, unequal masses.
3. Perfectly inelastic (they stick), equal masses.
4. An "explosion": carts pushed apart from rest by a compressed spring.

For each, record both masses and all velocities before and after,
including their signs.

## Analysis

For each collision, compute total momentum before and after, and total
kinetic energy before and after.

1. Which quantity was conserved every time? Which was not?
2. In the explosion, both carts move but the total momentum is zero. Is
   that consistent? Show it with your numbers.
3. For the inelastic collision, what fraction of the kinetic energy
   disappeared, and where did it go?
4. Your momentum totals will not balance exactly. Give the percentage,
   and name the two most likely reasons.
