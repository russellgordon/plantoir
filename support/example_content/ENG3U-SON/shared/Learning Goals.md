By the end of this course, you'll be able to do a handful of things that
sound simple stated plainly and take a whole semester to actually build:

- **Read something difficult and explain what it means and how it works** —
  not just what happened, but how the writer built the effect you felt.
- **Say something clearly to a room and listen well enough to respond to
  what someone else actually said**, not what you assumed they'd say.
- **Write with a real thesis, real evidence, and a voice that's yours** —
  across a short essay, a longer argument, and a piece meant to persuade.
- **Read and make the texts that aren't printed pages** — an ad, a
  headline, a podcast, a video — with the same critical eye you bring to a
  novel.

These four map onto the course's four strands, and the Ministry's own
wording for a few of them is below, for anyone who wants the exact language
a report card or a course outline draws from.
