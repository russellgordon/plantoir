A double circuit: one loop to the lungs, one to the body, with the heart
driving both.

## The path

Body → right atrium → right ventricle → lungs → left atrium → left
ventricle → body. Two pumps in one organ, which is why the left ventricle
wall is so much thicker — it pushes blood around the whole body rather
than to the lungs next door. That difference is obvious the moment you
hold a heart, which is the point of [[Heart Structure]].

## The vessels

| Vessel | Wall | Pressure | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artery | Thick, elastic | High | Carry away from the heart |
| Capillary | One cell thick | Low | Exchange |
| Vein | Thin, with valves | Low | Return, against gravity |

Capillaries are where the entire point of the system happens; everything
else is plumbing to and from them.

## Blood

Plasma, red cells (haemoglobin, oxygen transport), white cells (immunity),
platelets (clotting). One drop holds about five million red cells, and
they are replaced at roughly two million per second.
