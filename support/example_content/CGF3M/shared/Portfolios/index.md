Work built across the whole term rather than handed in on one day. This is
where the course's continuity lives: what you recorded at the start of the
course becomes evidence at the end of the course.

- [[Your Field Notebook]] — every field day, observation on the left and
  inference on the right
- [[A Map of Your Own]] — four maps, each with a reflection on what you
  chose to leave off
- [[Skills You Are Building]] — what this course is actually teaching
  you, and where else it is used
- [[Judging Your Own Work]] — how to read a draft of your own against the
  criteria while there is still time to change it

The first three are collected and marked as one piece of work,
[[The Field and Map Portfolio]], which says what earns the marks and when
each part is due. Keep them going from week one: a portfolio assembled in
the last fortnight is visible from a long way off.

The fourth carries no mark of its own and never will, because your
judgement of your own work is not part of anybody's grade. It is the
thing that makes the other three better.
