Your own record of how your thinking changed over the semester.

A journal is **chosen, not accumulated**. A handful of entries written
close to the work, while you still remember what confused you, beats a
folder containing everything you ever handed in.

Start with [[Science Journal]] for what this is and why we bother, then
write [[Your First Entry]] in the first week.
[[What a Strong Entry Looks Like]] shows you the standard, and
[[Journal Checklist]] is what I check against at the end.

[[Judging Your Own Work]] lives here too. It is not about the journal — it
is the fifteen-minute routine for holding a draft up against the criteria
published on the task page, which is the habit this folder exists to build.
