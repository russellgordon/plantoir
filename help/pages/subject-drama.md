This page lists what a Drama website made with Plantoir can show. Each item names a page of the ready-made Drama course where you can see it, and the Help page that says how it is done.

## The ready-made Drama course

One code has ready-made pages: **ADA1O**, Drama, Grade 9.

To get it, choose {{menu:newCourse}}, type ADA1O and leave {{control:newCourseSheetPrePopulateWithExampleContent}} turned on. [Make a new course](page:new-course) has the steps. It comes with its folders, its shared pages, and 86 class pages in four units for every section. The class pages are published and dated across the semester, so the first deploy may ask about classes dated later. See [Classes dated after the next class, at Deploy](page:later-classes).

To read it without teaching from it, put it on the Reference Courses shelf. See [Bring in last year's courses](page:import-for-reference).

Any other Drama code offers a starting point for the subject instead. Its folders are Concepts, Conventions, Warm-Ups, Discussions, Portfolios, Tasks, Setup, Style, Tutorials and Curriculum.

## What the pages show

- **Conventions and warm-ups, written once.** Each convention and each warm-up is one shared page, and class pages link to it. The page Tableau, in Conventions, is one. The class pages that link to it include Unit 1, Day 4, Unit 1, Day 8 and Unit 2, Day 21. See [One lesson, written once](page:one-lesson).
- **A video on a page.** The page Tableau has a part named Tableau on the professional stage. It holds an interview from Canada's Theatre Museum, in which the two founding directors of Opera Atelier, a Toronto company, talk about tableau. A credit line and three things to listen for follow it. Students see a YouTube player in the page.

{{picture:subject-drama-1}}
alt: The part named Tableau on the professional stage, on the page Tableau on the class website, with the interview's player and what to listen for.

The interview is not copied into the course. It plays from YouTube when a student opens the page, so it needs an internet connection. The page tells students so, under the interview. A video does not print.

On the page, the interview is its address on a line of its own, in the form used for a picture:

```text wrap
![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EIOxEqlYgXo)
```

- **A question students check themselves.** The page Thought Tracking, in Conventions, has one under the heading Check your ear. It describes a tableau of a family posed for a graduation photograph, and asks which of four lines is a thought and not a caption. A student picks one and presses {{control:quizCheck}}, or presses {{control:quizShowAnswer}}. An explanation named Why appears after the student answers. Nothing a student answers reaches you. See [Questions students check themselves](page:quizzes).

{{picture:subject-drama-2}}

- **A checklist students tick.** Every class page has one, under the heading Things to do before our next class. See [Checklists students tick](page:task-boxes).
- **Diagrams drawn from text.** The page Tableau Story Sequence, in Tasks, has one. The page What This Site Can Do, in Style, has a part named Diagrams, with examples named Flowchart, Proportions, Sequence and Timeline. See [Diagrams drawn from text](page:diagrams-from-text).

- **Curriculum expectations and a map of them.** The Curriculum folder has a page for every expectation. Lesson pages show the expectations they address, under the heading Curriculum connection. See [Curriculum expectations and the coverage map](page:coverage).

## Folders made for the subject

- **Conventions** has eight pages: Tableau, Hot Seating, Thought Tracking, Mime, Choral Speaking, Corridor of Voices, Flashback and Flash-Forward, and Writing in Role.
- **Warm-Ups** has eight: Zip Zap Zop, Mirrors, Status Walks, Soundscape, Machine, Group Counting, Yes Lets and What Are You Doing.
- **Style** holds How This Site Is Organised, What This Site Can Do and Writing About Drama.
- **Tasks** is the folder whose work counts for marks.

[The folders Plantoir treats specially](page:special-folders) says what the folder for marks does.

## What ADA1O does not use

ADA1O shows no picture, has no page that prints as a handout, and has no success criteria for students to tick. A page can hold each all the same. See [Pictures, PDFs and the Media folder](page:media), [A handout that prints](page:printing) and [Let students track their progress](page:my-progress).
