Every class opens with five minutes of news judgement — no cameras,
no computers, no marks. Just the room, a prompt on the screen, and
the discipline working editors live by: deciding fast what a reader
needs and saying why out loud, long before deadline week makes it real.

| Warm-up | What it trains |
| --- | --- |
| [[Caption This]] | Who, what, and when — in two lines |
| [[Headline Help Desk]] | Verbs and specifics, honest but alive |
| [[News or Not]] | News judgement, defended out loud |
| [[Spot the Edit]] | Seeing what changed, and whether it lies |
| [[Which One Doesn't Belong]] | The vocabulary of craft, argued |
| [[One-Minute Pitch]] | Selling a story on its merits, fast |

When a round starts feeling easy, that is the habit landing — the fix
is sharper specimens, not higher stakes.
