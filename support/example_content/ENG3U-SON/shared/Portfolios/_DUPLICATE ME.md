## What belongs in Portfolios

Reflection and self-assessment — pages that build a student's judgement
about their own work, never pages that themselves get marked. [[Judging Your Own Work]],
[[Your Reading Life]], and [[Looking Back, Looking Ahead]]
are the three examples already here.

## Reminders

- **Nothing on a Portfolios page ever feeds a mark.** Growing Success is
  explicit that a student's own judgement of their work, or a peer's,
  cannot be part of an evaluation — see the policy note on
  [[How Marks Work]]. If you're tempted to attach a grade to something
  here, it belongs in Tasks instead, and it needs a different kind of page.
- **Link it from a class page**, directly or through one page a class page
  already links to.
- If the page is meant to be used more than once across the course — the
  way [[Judging Your Own Work]] is — say on the page itself where its FIRST
  modelled use happens, and let later class pages just point back to it.
- Curriculum-expectation transclusions are optional here; most reflection
  pages won't need one, but if this page genuinely teaches or evidences a
  specific expectation, add the block the same way any content page does.

Preview your change (⌘+E in Obsidian toggles Reading view) before you
