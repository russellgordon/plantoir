A bound notebook, used in ink, in order, without pages torn out. That
combination is what makes it a **record** rather than a collection of notes.

## Every entry starts with

```
Date · Investigation title · Group members
```

## While working

- Write readings **as they happen**, not from memory afterwards
- Mistake? One line through it, initial, keep going. Never scribble out
- Record the unexpected — the smell, the odd bubble, the reading that made no
  sense
- Sketch the setup. A rough sketch beats a paragraph

> [!important] Why ink and why bound
> Because a lab notebook is evidence. In research and in industry, notebooks
> settle patent disputes and safety investigations. The habits are the same at
> every level: contemporaneous, unaltered, honest.

## What a good one looks like

Not neatness. **Completeness and honesty** — including the trial that failed and
what you did next.

The notebook itself is not marked. What it does is make the seven marked
[[Lab Reports]] possible: everything you write while the work is happening is
what you have to write from afterwards, and the criterion about naming your
limits honestly is more or less impossible without it.
