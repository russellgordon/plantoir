## Agenda

1. Standup
2. Warm-up: three failed prints on the table — spaghetti, a warped
   corner, a stringy tower. What went wrong with each? Guesses first
3. Name it: [[3D Printing]] — layers, infill, supports, and why prints
   fail
4. Studio: [[The Tolerance Coupon]] — three coupons, two rounds of
   crews, about twenty minutes; everyone measures and records
   (formative)
5. The clearance goes up on the board, and back into
   [[Doing the Numbers]] if anyone's millimetres and inches have got
   tangled
