## Before you ask

Bring the sentence: **"I expected X, I did Y, I got Z."** It turns twenty
minutes of hunting into two. If you cannot fill in X, that is itself the
problem, and a good place to start.

## In class

Put your hand up. If I am with another group, start writing down what you
have tried — half the time you will find it yourself while writing, and if
not, you have the sentence above ready when I arrive.

## Outside class

[[Help Sessions]] has the times. You do not need a question prepared;
sitting in the same room as other people doing biology is a legitimate
reason to come, and it is how most people use it.

## From each other

Explaining a problem to someone else is the fastest way to find out
whether you understand it. This is allowed, encouraged, and not cheating —
copying an answer is, and it is obvious from a mile away, because copied
work has no crossings-out.
