Stuck is a normal newsroom condition — [[Getting Help]] says so, and
working reporters live there weekly. But there are two kinds of
stuck. **Stuck-and-thinking** is trying changes and judging them; it
looks slow and is actually progress. **Spinning** is rereading the
same draft hoping it reads differently this time. It will not.
These moves convert spinning back into thinking:

1. **Read it aloud.** The ear catches what the eye forgives: the
   lead that clears its throat for two sentences, the quote that
   does not say what you remembered it saying, the sentence you
   cannot finish in one breath.
2. **Shrink the story.** Tell it in one sentence to the person
   beside you. If you cannot, the problem is not the writing — it is
   that you have not decided what the story is, which is a
   [[The Inverted Pyramid]] problem wearing a writing costume.
3. **Call one more source.** Most stuck stories are under-reported,
   not under-written. One more voice — the coach, the organiser, the
   person affected — usually hands you the missing paragraph, per
   [[Interviewing]].
4. **Look at it at thumbnail size.** For photos and layouts: shrink
   it until the detail disappears. What survives small is the
   composition; if nothing survives, that is your answer — see
   [[Composition and Framing]].
5. **Change one thing at a time.** In the shop, a failed print or a
   burnt edge has several possible causes. Change one setting, run the
   smallest test that will show the difference, and write down what
   happened — two changes at once teach you nothing about either, and
   the log stops the same failure happening to the next crew.
6. **Ask your desk.** Standup exists for exactly this. "I have the
   quotes but no lead" is a professional sentence, not a confession,
   and someone at your desk has been there this week.

## When to file what you have

Honest test: *when did you last change something on purpose?* If the
answer is "three reads ago", you are spinning — pick a move above or
escalate through [[Getting Help]]. And when the deadline arrives
mid-stuck, file what you have with the weakness flagged for your
editor, per [[Filing a Story]]. A flagged gap is workable; a missed
deadline is a hole on someone else's page.
