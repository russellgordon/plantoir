> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class. **Groups of three.** The evidence table is used in
> [[An Evolution Case Study]].

Photographs and models of five vertebrate forelimbs: human, cat, whale,
bat, bird.

## Method

1. For each limb, identify and colour the humerus, radius, ulna, carpals,
   metacarpals, and phalanges. Use the same colour for the same bone
   across all five.
2. Record the function of each limb.
3. Build a table: bone present, relative size, function.

## Analysis

1. Which bones appear in all five, and in the same ORDER? What does that
   pattern support?
2. A bat's wing and an insect's wing do the same job. Are they homologous?
   Justify from structure, not from function.
3. Whales have a pelvis and femur reduced to fragments that connect to
   nothing. What is the simplest explanation, and what would a competing
   explanation have to account for?
4. Which single specimen would most change your conclusion if it were
   removed? That question is about how evidence works, not about whales.
