Every page in this folder names an idea you first wrestled with at the
boards — in a visibly random group, marker in hand, before anyone told
you what it was called. That order is deliberate: the wrestling builds
the understanding, and these pages consolidate it, written for your
future forgetful self. Read them after the exploration, never instead
of it.
