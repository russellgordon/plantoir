## Agenda

1. Read a piece of flash fiction together, in class, out loud — barely a
   page long: a teenager walks into a summer-job interview planning to lie
   about experience they don't have, and the interview doesn't go the way
   either of them expects. No vocabulary yet — just read it and notice what
   you notice.
2. Talk, as a class, about the person at the centre of it: what do you
   already know about them, and how do you know it, without the story ever
   stating it outright?
3. Name what you were just doing: [[Character and Characterization]]
4. Name the shape the story took, start to finish: [[Conflict and Plot Structure]]
