Two periods, working on your own for the first and with a partner for
the second. The output is a list of things you can do, each one attached
to a moment when you did it. Nobody marks the list itself; it feeds
[[The Skills Inventory Task]], and it feeds everything after that.

## Period one — the raw list

Set a timer for twelve minutes and write down every situation you can
remember where you finished something, fixed something, taught someone,
organised something, or kept going when it would have been easier to stop.
Situations, not adjectives. "Reliable" is an adjective and proves nothing;
"covered three shifts nobody else would take at the end of the course" is a
situation and proves several things.

Draw from all of it: classes, teams, clubs, jobs, volunteering, family
responsibilities, looking after a sibling, running a group chat that
actually organised something, a game you got genuinely good at.

Then go back through and, beside each situation, name the skill it
demonstrates. Use the language from [[Skills That Transfer]] where it
fits, and your own words where it does not.

## Period two — the partner pass

Swap lists. Your partner's job is to ask one question of every line:
**how would someone else know that?** If the answer is "they would have
to take my word for it", the line needs a better example or it comes
off the list.

Then each of you names two things about the other that they did not
write down. People are consistently poor at seeing their own ordinary
competence, and this is the fastest fix available.

## What to end up with

Copy this into your notebook — the boxes below are printed, not
clickable, and nothing here is recorded.

- [ ] At least twelve situations, dated at least roughly
- [ ] A skill named beside each one
- [ ] Every skill backed by something a third person could verify
- [ ] Two skills your partner saw and you did not
- [ ] Three skills you would like to have and currently cannot evidence

That last line is not a weakness section. It is the start of your plan,
and it is what the second half of this course is for.
