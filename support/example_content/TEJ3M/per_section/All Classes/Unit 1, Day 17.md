## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Name That Part]] — the graveyard round, failed components
   only. What killed each one, and what would the meter have shown?
2. Bench time: [[The Working Circuit]] finished — the weakest row you
   named last class first — then demonstrated: measured current,
   measured drops, and one resistor's temperature after five minutes of
   running
3. Compare and name it: [[Power and Heat]] — watts, part ratings, and
   why warm is fine and hot is a warning
4. Practise: [[Power Calculations Practice]], first block together
5. Hand in [[The Working Circuit]] — board, schematic, calculation
   sheet and measurement sheet — with the [[Journal Checklist]] open
   beside you for the journal as well
