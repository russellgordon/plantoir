## What belongs in Tutorials

Tutorials are about the SITE, not the course — how to find something, how a
feature works, how to navigate. [[Using This Site]] and [[Scavenger Hunt]]
are the two examples already here. If what you're writing explains a
literary idea instead, it belongs in **Concepts**, not here.

## A starting shape

```markdown
---
title: Your Title Here
publish: true
created: __CREATED__
tags:
  - tutorials
---
One or two sentences on what this page helps a student do.

## First heading

## Second heading
```

Only add `enableToc: true` if the page ends up with four or more `##`
headings — fewer than that, a table of contents is clutter, not help.

## Reminders

- **Link it from a class page**, directly or through one page a class page
  already links to — nothing on this site stands alone. If there is no
  honest place in the schedule to link a new tutorial from, that is a sign
  the page may not need to exist yet.
- Tutorials never carry curriculum-expectation transclusions — that belongs
  on Concepts, Texts, Discussions, Writing Workshops, Media Studies, or
  Tasks pages, wherever a student is actually doing the work an expectation
  describes.
- Keep the voice direct and second person, the same as every other page on
  this site — you are talking to the student reading it, not describing the
  software to a third party.

Preview your change (⌘+E in Obsidian toggles Reading view) before you
consider the page finished.
