About half of Ontario's electricity comes from CANDU reactors, most of
them within 100 km of where you are sitting. That makes this a local
question, not a philosophical one.

## The physics you need first

[[Nuclear Energy]] — fission, chain reactions, half-life, and why spent
fuel is dangerous for a very long time. Also the arithmetic that makes
nuclear compelling: the energy released per kilogram of fuel is about a
million times that of coal.

## The three honest positions

1. **Expand it.** Low emissions, high output, proven here.
2. **Maintain but do not expand.** The waste problem is unsolved and the
   build cost is enormous.
3. **Wind it down.** Renewables plus storage, accepting the land use.

Each has a real cost. Anyone arguing a position must state theirs.

## The question that usually turns the room

What would have to be true for you to change your mind? If nothing would,
the position is not about physics.
