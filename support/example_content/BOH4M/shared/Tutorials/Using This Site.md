## The three things worth knowing

**Links go both ways.** At the bottom of most pages is a list of the
pages that link to it. If you land on [[Conflict Styles]] and want to
know where it was used, that list is the answer.

**Search is fast.** Press `Ctrl`+`K` (or `Cmd`+`K`) and type. It searches
the words inside pages, not only titles.

**A folded block hides something.** A line with a small arrow opens when
you click it — usually a check-your-understanding, or a worked answer.
Try to answer first; reading the answer feels like learning and is not.

> [!note] Checkboxes here are yours
> The `- [ ]` lists on class pages can be ticked as you work through them.
> Your browser keeps the ticks on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere
> and the site has no idea who you are.

## If something is missing

A class page appears the day we get to it. If a link on today's page does
not work yet, it is because tomorrow has not happened. Ask, and I will
say whether it is deliberate.
