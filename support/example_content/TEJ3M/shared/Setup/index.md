Everything you need to know before the tools come out. Read these in
your first week, and come back whenever a routine feels fuzzy. If you
took the Grade 10 course, the page names are deliberately identical —
the room is the same room. What is behind the names has moved up a
level, because the equipment has.

- [[How the Lab Runs]] — the shop rhythm: warm up, safety notes, bench
  time, compare and name, tools away and log.
- [[Safety in the Lab]] — the four absolutes, and safety as
  professional practice rather than a list of prohibitions. The most
  important page in this folder, and it is not close.
- [[Our Classroom Norms]] — predict with a number, measure before you
  argue, dead circuits welcome, help always credited.
- [[What to Wear and Bring]] — closed toes, tied hair, eye protection,
  a calculator, and a paper journal.
- [[How Marks Work]] — the seventy and the thirty, the four kinds of
  thinking, and the three kinds of evidence. Not whether you are "good
  with your hands", because that is learned too.
- [[Getting Help]] — measure before you guess, and report every
  incident.

> [!important] Two of these are agreements, not information
> [[Safety in the Lab]] and [[Our Classroom Norms]] bind everyone in
> the room, including your teacher. We walk the lab together in the
> first week and finalise both, and every lab page links back to them
> for the whole semester.
