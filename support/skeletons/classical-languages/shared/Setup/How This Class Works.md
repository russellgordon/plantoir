## The rhythm of a class

Most classes in this course follow the same shape, so you always know what
is coming:

1. Warm-up: two minutes of talk, no notes
2. New vocabulary, in a sentence you would actually say — [[Readings/index|Readings]]
3. Read together, and listen once without the text — [[Readings/index|Readings]]
4. Practise in pairs — [[Translation/index|Translation]]
5. Share: one sentence each, out loud

## What to do when you are stuck

Ask in class, ask at [[Help Sessions]], or check [[Getting Help]]. Being
stuck is ordinary. Staying stuck quietly is the only mistake.
