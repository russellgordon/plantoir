The expectations transcluded throughout this course come from *The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 11 and 12: English*, Revised (2007), © Queen's Printer for Ontario, 2007. Official course page: https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/secondary-english/courses/eng3u-pdf. Source document: https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/english1112currb.pdf (expectations for ENG3U appear on printed pages 41-57, PDF pages 42-58).

In effect since September 2007, replacing the 2000 edition. Grade 9 English was separately revised in 2023 (ENL1W); that revision does not touch Grades 10-12, so this 2007 document remains the current, live source governing ENG3U.

## Strands

- **A. Oral Communication**
- **B. Reading and Literature Studies**
- **C. Writing**
- **D. Media Studies**

Each strand groups a small number of OVERALL expectations — broad statements of what a student will be able to do by the end of the course — with several SPECIFIC expectations under each, describing that ability in more detail. A code like A1.2 names the first strand's first overall expectation, second specific expectation.

Where the ministry's own document pairs a specific expectation with a bracketed "(e.g., ...)" example, that example is folded into the expectation's page verbatim, in the same sentence — it is Ministry text too, not commentary added here. The document also attaches one or more "Teacher prompt" questions to most specific expectations; those are a separate field in the source and are not reproduced on these pages, to keep each expectation page to the expectation itself.

Two general-education footnote markers threaded through the specific expectations (references to the Ministry's "Think Literacy Library" teacher-resource guides) have been omitted from these pages for the same reason — they are citation apparatus pointing teachers to external guides, not part of the expectation's own wording.
