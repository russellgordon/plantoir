This page lists what a Dance website made with Plantoir can show. Each item names a page of the ready-made Dance course where you can see it, and the Help page that says how it is done. It begins with videos.

## The ready-made Dance course

One code has ready-made pages: **ATC1O**, Dance, Grade 9.

To get it, choose {{menu:newCourse}}, type ATC1O and leave {{control:newCourseSheetPrePopulateWithExampleContent}} turned on. [Make a new course](page:new-course) has the steps. It comes with its folders, its shared pages, and 86 class pages in four units for every section. The class pages are dated across the semester. All but the last are published, and the last is held back as an example of a page students do not see yet. So the first deploy may ask about classes dated later. See [Classes dated after the next class, at Deploy](page:later-classes).

To read it without teaching from it, put it on the Reference Courses shelf. See [Bring in last year's courses](page:import-for-reference).

Any other Dance code offers a starting point for the subject instead. Its folders are Concepts, Technique, Warm-Ups, Choreography, Portfolios, Tasks, Setup, Style, Tutorials and Curriculum.

## What the pages show

The page Four Forms on Film, in Technique, holds four films. Each is a lesson in one form of dance, and each plays in the page.

The page has one part for each form:

- **Ballet.** A beginner class for adults from Canada's National Ballet School.
- **Jazz, at its roots.** A lesson in the Charleston from the Kennedy Center.
- **Hip hop.** A lesson in breaking from a teaching artist in Seattle.
- **Bharatanatyam.** A film on the ways of moving the legs and feet, from India's National Council of Educational Research and Training.

Each part has a sentence about the film, then the film, then two questions. Before the four parts, a part named How to watch gives four questions to watch any of the films by. The page tells students to pick one film and not to watch all four in one sitting.

- **Students see a YouTube player** where each address is.
- **A film is not copied into the course.** The player loads from YouTube when a student opens the page, and the film plays when the student presses play. So the student needs an internet connection.
- **The page tells students so.** It ends with a note named About these films. The note says that each film plays from YouTube, needs an internet connection and will not print. It adds that the four makers published their films under a Creative Commons Attribution licence.
- **Watch each film through** before your class does. The films were chosen from what their YouTube pages say: the licence, the publisher, and that they can be shown in another page. They were not watched through when the course was written. The ballet class runs close to an hour.

The class page for Unit 3, Day 3 links to Four Forms on Film in its agenda. So does the page Forms From Around the World, in Technique.

A video file of your own is a different thing. It goes in the course's Media folder. See [Pictures, PDFs and the Media folder](page:media).

The pages also show these:

- **Pages written once and linked from class pages.** The pages in Technique, Warm-Ups and Choreography are each written once. Class pages link to them. The Standard Warm-Up, in Warm-Ups, is the first item in the agenda for Unit 3, Day 3. See [One lesson, written once](page:one-lesson).
- **A checklist students tick.** Every class page has one, under the heading Things to do before our next class. See [Checklists students tick](page:task-boxes).
- **Diagrams drawn from text.** Six pages that students see have one. Among them are The Standard Warm-Up, in Warm-Ups, Three Forms We Practise, in Technique, and The Dance Portfolio, in Portfolios. See [Diagrams drawn from text](page:diagrams-from-text).
- **Tables, boxes and footnotes.** The page What This Site Can Do, in Style, shows each with examples from the course. See [Tables, boxes and footnotes](page:tables-boxes-footnotes).

## Folders made for the subject

- **Technique** has six pages. They are A Technique Class, Alignment and Placement, Forms From Around the World, Four Forms on Film, Learning a Sequence and Three Forms We Practise.
- **Warm-Ups** has five: Cool-Down and Recovery, Improvisation Warm-Ups, Partner Warm-Ups, The Standard Warm-Up and Warm-Up as Choreography.
- **Choreography** has five: Devising in a Group, Phrase Building, Showing and Feedback, Structures for Making, and With Music and Without.
- **Style** holds How This Site Is Organised and What This Site Can Do.
- **Tasks** is the folder whose work counts for marks.

[The folders Plantoir treats specially](page:special-folders) says what the folder for marks does.

## What ATC1O does not use

ATC1O has no question that students check themselves, no picture, no page that prints as a handout, and no success criteria for students to tick. A page can hold each all the same. See [Questions students check themselves](page:quizzes), [Pictures, PDFs and the Media folder](page:media), [A handout that prints](page:printing) and [Let students track their progress](page:my-progress).
