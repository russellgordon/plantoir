## Agenda

1. Standup — prototypes cut in the queue order on the board
2. Cardboard prototypes, nested four to a sheet: six sheets, about 36
   laser minutes, the person whose sheet is cutting at the machine
   throughout. Hand-cut from a printed template if your slot slips
3. Test with your user: stand where they will stand, measure the
   distance, and check every criterion
4. Name it: [[Quality Control]] — a standard, a check, a record
5. File and log: exactly what the test found, in your
   [[Newsroom Journal]] (formative — I read these tonight)
