A page for whatever you want to keep track of that doesn't belong on a
public page — seating notes, reminders to yourself about a class's pacing,
anything else that is useful to write down and not useful for a student to
read.

Nothing here is linked from anywhere else on the site, and nothing here is
meant to be. Write in it however works for you.
