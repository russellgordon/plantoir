> [!abstract] At a glance
> Individual · built across the whole semester, entry by entry, and assembled
> in class on Unit 5, Days 13 and 14 · handed in at the end of Day 14 ·
> **part of the final 30%**, with [[Culminating Reflection]] and the
> [[Final Examination]]
>
> **Success criteria:** the four rows on [[Portfolio Checklist]], which you
> read in the first week of the course and have had ever since.

Bring everything together into one place a reader can move through.

## Structure

1. **A short introduction** — who you were at the start of the course, in three
   sentences.
2. **The work**, roughly chronological, each piece introduced by why it is here.
3. **[[Culminating Reflection]]** as the closing.

## The one instruction that matters

Write it for a reader who was not in the room. Not for me — I was there. Imagine
a student taking this course next year, reading yours to find out what it is
like.

> [!question] A good test before submitting
> Would the version of you from the first week have found this useful? If yes, you
> have written something worth keeping.
