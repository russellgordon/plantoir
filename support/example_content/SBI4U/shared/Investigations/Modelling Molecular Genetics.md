> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class. **Pairs.** Every mutation type, done by hand.

Paper models of transcription and translation, then deliberate damage.

## Method

1. Build a 15-base template strand and transcribe it to mRNA.
2. Translate with a codon chart to get a five-amino-acid peptide.
3. Now introduce, one at a time, on fresh copies:
   - a silent substitution;
   - a missense substitution;
   - a nonsense substitution;
   - a single-base deletion.
4. Re-translate each and record the resulting peptide.

## Analysis

1. Rank your four mutations by damage done. Justify the ranking.
2. Why can a substitution be silent at all? Answer with the redundancy of
   the code from [[Transcription and Translation]].
3. Why is the deletion so much worse than any substitution?
4. Sickle-cell disease is a single missense mutation. Given your results,
   explain why one substitution can be so consequential when most are not.
