## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Visual Patterns]] — squares of dots, growing: how big
   is each jump, and why?
2. Random groups: the derivatives of $x$, $x^2$, and $x^3$ on one
   board, your $x^{17}$ conjectures beside them — find the machine.
   Those conjectures are this unit's diagnostic: which of you
   generalises from a pattern, and which wants a proof first, sets the
   pace of the next two weeks
3. Counter-examples invited: does the machine survive $x^{50}$? A
   constant? $4x^3 + x$?
4. Consolidate: [[Derivative Rules]] — power, constant multiple, sum,
   difference: conjectured first, stated second
5. Practise: [[Derivative Rules Practice]], first set
