## In order of speed

1. **Ask in class.** If you are confused, three other people are too.
2. **Check the concept page.** Most "I don't get it" moments are answered there.
3. **Come to a help session.** Times are on [[Help Sessions]].
4. **Email me.** Include what you tried — it makes the answer far more useful.

## What to say instead of "I don't get it"

- "I understand X, but I get lost at Y."
- "I expected A and got B."
- "I tried this and it did not work — here is what I did."

Each of those gets you a real answer in one exchange rather than four.

> [!note] Ask early
> Science builds. A gap in circuits in week 3 becomes a wall in week 6. Nobody
> has ever annoyed me by asking a question too early.
