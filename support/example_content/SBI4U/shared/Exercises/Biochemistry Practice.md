**1.** Explain why ice floats, and give one biological consequence.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Hydrogen bonds hold water molecules in an open lattice when frozen, so
> ice is less dense than liquid water. Lakes freeze from the top down, so
> organisms survive beneath the ice.

**2.** Starch and cellulose are both glucose polymers. Why can you digest
one and not the other?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> The glycosidic bonds differ in orientation ($\alpha\text{-1,4}$ vs
> $\beta\text{-1,4}$). Human enzymes fit the bonds in starch and not those
> in cellulose — an active-site shape argument.

**3.** A protein is heated to 80 °C and loses its function. Which level of
structure was lost first, and why does the primary structure survive?

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Tertiary (and secondary), held by relatively weak hydrogen bonds, ionic
> interactions, and hydrophobic associations. Primary structure is held by
> covalent peptide bonds, which survive this temperature.

**4.** Why does a phospholipid form a bilayer in water without anything
arranging it?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> The hydrophilic phosphate heads interact favourably with water while the
> hydrophobic hydrocarbon tails are excluded by it, so the lowest-energy
> arrangement buries the tails inward.

**5.** A competitive inhibitor's effect can be overcome by adding more
substrate; a non-competitive inhibitor's cannot. Why?

> [!success]- Answer 5
> Competitive inhibitors compete directly for the active site, so higher
> substrate concentration out-competes them. Non-competitive inhibitors bind
> at an allosteric site and alter the enzyme's conformation, so additional
> substrate cannot restore the reaction rate.

**6.** In the dairy industry, lactase is immobilised on beads to produce
lactose-free milk. What are two advantages of immobilising the enzyme
rather than adding it directly to the milk?

> [!success]- Answer 6
> 1. The enzyme can be recovered and reused continuously, significantly
>    lowering industrial production costs.
> 2. The finished product remains free of enzyme contamination, avoiding
>    the need for downstream separation or heat inactivation.

**7.** Potato cores of equal mass are placed in distilled water (0.0 M) and
a 0.6 M sucrose solution for 60 minutes. Predict the percentage mass change
in each and explain the movement of water across the membrane.

> [!success]- Answer 7
> In distilled water (hypotonic), water moves into the cells by osmosis down
> its water potential gradient, causing mass to increase (turgor). In 0.6 M
> sucrose (hypertonic), water moves out of the cells into the solution,
> causing mass to decrease (plasmolysis).

**8.** Classify each reaction as condensation, hydrolysis, redox, or
neutralization:
a. Two amino acids joining to form a dipeptide and water.
b. A triglyceride reacting with three water molecules to yield glycerol and
fatty acids.
c. $\ce{NAD+ + 2H+ + 2e- -> NADH + H+}$.
d. $\ce{H2CO3 + NaOH -> NaHCO3 + H2O}$.

> [!success]- Answer 8
> a. Condensation (dehydration synthesis).
> b. Hydrolysis.
> c. Oxidation-reduction (redox / reduction of NAD⁺).
> d. Neutralization (acid-base buffering).
