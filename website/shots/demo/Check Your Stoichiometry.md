Three quick questions on [[Stoichiometry]]. Molar masses to two decimal
places; round once, at the end. Your score is at the bottom of the page.

> [!quiz] Propane burns in plenty of oxygen: $\ce{C3H8(g) + 5O2(g) -> 3CO2(g) + 4H2O(g)}$. Burning 4.40 g of propane uses `____` mol of propane and makes `____` g of carbon dioxide.
> > [!answer]-
> > 0.0998 ± 0.0005
> > 13.2 ± 0.1
>
> > [!explain]- Why
> > Mass to moles: $n = \frac{4.40\ \text{g}}{44.11\ \text{g/mol}} \approx 0.09975\ \text{mol}$ of propane.
> > Moles to moles, from the balanced equation: three $\ce{CO2}$ for every
> > one $\ce{C3H8}$, so $3 \times 0.09975 \approx 0.2993\ \text{mol}$.
> > Moles to mass: $0.2993\ \text{mol} \times 44.01\ \text{g/mol} \approx 13.17\ \text{g}$,
> > which rounds to 13.2 g of carbon dioxide. An answer of 4.39 g skipped
> > the middle step.

> [!quiz|shuffle] Which equation for the burning of methane is balanced?
> - [x] $\ce{CH4(g) + 2O2(g) -> CO2(g) + 2H2O(g)}$
> - [ ] $\ce{CH4(g) + O2(g) -> CO2(g) + H2O(g)}$
> - [ ] $\ce{CH4(g) + 2O2(g) -> CO2(g) + H2O(g)}$
> - [ ] $\ce{CH4(g) + 3O2(g) -> CO2(g) + 2H2O(g)}$
>
> > [!explain]- Why
> > Count each element on both sides: one C, four H and four O.

> [!quiz] Which are products of the complete combustion of a hydrocarbon?
> - [x] $\ce{CO2(g)}$
> - [x] $\ce{H2O(g)}$
> - [ ] $\ce{CO(g)}$
> - [ ] $\ce{C(s)}$
>
> > [!explain]- Why
> > With enough oxygen every carbon ends as carbon dioxide and every
> > hydrogen as water. Carbon monoxide and soot come from incomplete
> > combustion, when oxygen runs short.
