**The question:** where is the edge? Some movement is obviously dancing and
some obviously is not — so what happens in between?

This seminar starts seated and ends on its feet. When words run out, stand up
and demonstrate — a demonstration settles some disputes and starts better
ones.

## Prepare

Bring **one** case from the edge, something you are genuinely unsure of, and
be ready to perform ten seconds of it. Then bring a one-sentence rule that
would decide your case, and test your rule against somebody else's case before
you arrive. Most rules die in about four seconds.

## Cases to have ready

| The case | The question it forces |
| --- | --- |
| A figure skating programme | Is a scoring system incompatible with dancing? |
| A goal celebration | Does something count if nobody planned it? |
| Fifteen seconds of a trend, filmed alone | Is an audience of strangers still an audience? |
| Walking across the studio in silence | Can framing alone turn action into dance? |
| A procession at a ceremony | Is dancing something a group does, or something it means? |
| A warm-up | Does purpose decide it — preparing versus performing? |

## Three positions

**Intent decides.** It is dancing when the movement is done for its own sake —
to be shaped, watched, or felt — rather than to accomplish something else. The
skater is scoring; the celebrant is celebrating; the trend is dancing.

**Form decides.** It is dancing when the movement is organised in time and
space with the qualities you can name — shape, rhythm, energy, relationship.
Use [[The Elements of Dance]] as the instrument. If all five are being worked
on purpose, the label follows, whoever is doing it and why.

**Context decides.** It is dancing when the people involved treat it as
dancing. No definition survives every culture, and a form one community calls
worship and another calls performance is not settled in a Grade 9 studio.

And a fourth: the boundary may not be worth defending. Nothing about your
practice changes on either side of it — so is this a real argument, or a
question about words? Say so if you think so, but then say why it kept coming
up all semester.

This is [[Content and Interpretation]] from the other side: content alone
rarely settles what something is. Whatever you decide should still hold when
you build [[The Movement Signature]] out of ordinary gestures.
