Most of what happens in a tech course evaporates as it happens —
programs get fixed and the broken versions vanish, and with them the
best evidence of your learning. Your journal is the part that stays:
the running record where every crashed build, pasted traceback, and
slow breakthrough turns into something you can look back through and
actually *see* changing. In this course, the journal is the
portfolio — the place broken builds become visible growth.

The hub is [[Dev Journal]]. The pages below support it, and the
judging page beside it, roughly in the order you meet them:

| Page | When you need it |
| --- | --- |
| [[Your First Entry]] | Week one, facing a blank page |
| [[What a Strong Entry Looks Like]] | Once the habit exists |
| [[Judging Your Own Work]] | A period or two before anything is due |
| [[Journal Checklist]] | Before each unit's collection |
| [[Showing Growth]] | Mid-course, when old entries become evidence |
| [[Final Reflection]] | The last week, reading the whole record |

> [!tip] Written forwards, read backwards
> Entries take ten minutes and feel small. The value arrives months
> later, when [[Showing Growth]] calls September-you as a witness —
> and that only works if September wrote honestly.
