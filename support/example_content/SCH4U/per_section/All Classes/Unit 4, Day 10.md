## Agenda

1. Titrate into water and into the mixture, plotting as you go — coarse
   at first, then drop by drop once the pH starts to move
2. Compare the two curves and mark where each one turns steepest
3. Name it: [[Buffers and Titration Curves]] — the pair doing the work
4. Task launch: [[The Buffer Design]] — a target pH and a defence of it;
   read the criteria table before you choose your context
5. If the calculation stalls on you: [[Getting Unstuck]]
