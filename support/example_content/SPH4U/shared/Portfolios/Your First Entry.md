Your first [[Physics Journal]] entry is started in class, on the day
[[The Flying Pig]] flies, so that the format is never a mystery
afterwards. Like every entry, it is not marked.

## The prompt

After [[The Flying Pig]], write three quarters of a page:

1. **In your own words:** why is there no outward force on the pig? Do not
   use the word "centrifugal" except to say what it is not.
2. **A calculation:** your predicted and measured speeds, and the percent
   difference, with one sentence on which you trust more.
3. **Still murky:** one question you would ask if there were no cost to
   asking it.

## What a good third question looks like

- Weak: "I do not get circular motion."
- Better: "If the net force points to the centre, why does the pig not
  spiral inward?"
- Best: "The mass cancelled in the derivation, but a heavier pig sagged to
  a bigger angle. Which assumption did that break?"

The last one is a genuinely good physics question — it noticed that a
propeller-driven pig is not quite the idealised bob in the derivation.
