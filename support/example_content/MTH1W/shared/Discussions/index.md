Some days this class puts the markers down and just talks. That is not
a break from mathematics — it is mathematics. A conjecture is a social
object: it only becomes knowledge after other people push on it, and
the pushing is done with questions, counterexamples, and "what if" —
never with anything aimed at the person who floated it. Disagree with
the idea; be glad someone was brave enough to say it out loud.

Each page below opens a conversation we return to all semester:

- [[When Will I Use This]] — the oldest question in math class, taken
  seriously and answered honestly
- [[Mistakes Are Data]] — why first attempts never get erased
- [[Why Struggle Is the Point]] — what hard thinking actually does
- [[Who Does Data Serve]] — the mathematics being done *about* you

Come with opinions. Leave with better ones.
