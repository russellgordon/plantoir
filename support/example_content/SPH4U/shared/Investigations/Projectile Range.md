> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class. **Pairs.** Written up next period as the unmarked
> practice run for [[Investigation Write-Ups]]. Prediction submitted
> BEFORE the test shot — that is the whole design.

A launcher, an angle, and one shot to prove you understand independence of
motion.

## Safety

Goggles on, launcher clamped, nobody downrange. Load only on the
instructor's word, and never look into the barrel.

## Method

1. Determine the launch speed. Fire horizontally from a measured height,
   measure the range, and work backwards — the vertical motion gives the
   time, the horizontal gives the speed.
2. Repeat five times; the spread in your launch speed is your dominant
   uncertainty.
3. Now predict the range at $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$, and $60^\circ$.
   Submit predictions with uncertainties before firing.
4. Fire and measure.

## Analysis

1. Did $45^\circ$ give the greatest range? Should it, given that the
   launch and landing heights are not the same?
2. Do $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ agree with each other? Explain from the
   equations.
3. Where does air resistance enter, and which angle does it hurt most?
