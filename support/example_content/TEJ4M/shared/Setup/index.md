Everything you need to know before the tools come out. Read these in
your first week, and come back whenever a routine feels fuzzy. If you
took the Grade 10 or Grade 11 course, the page names are deliberately
identical — the room is the same room, and a routine you can already
run is a routine you do not have to think about. What is behind the
names has moved up a level, because this year you are designing the
things other people will switch on.

- [[How the Lab Runs]] — the shop rhythm: warm up, safety notes,
  specify and predict, bench time, design review, tools away and log.
- [[Safety in the Lab]] — the absolutes, plus the safety decisions that
  now live in your designs rather than on your bench. The most
  important page in this folder, and it is not close.
- [[Our Classroom Norms]] — specify before you build, measure before
  you argue, critique the design and never the designer, help always
  credited.
- [[What to Wear and Bring]] — closed toes, tied hair, eye protection,
  a calculator, and a paper journal.
- [[How Marks Work]] — the seventy and the thirty, the four kinds of
  thinking, and what is deliberately kept out of your mark.
- [[Getting Help]] — measure before you guess, and report every
  incident.

> [!important] Two of these are agreements, not information
> [[Safety in the Lab]] and [[Our Classroom Norms]] bind everyone in
> the room, including your teacher. We walk the lab together in the
> first week and finalise both, and every lab and task page links back
> to them for the whole semester.
