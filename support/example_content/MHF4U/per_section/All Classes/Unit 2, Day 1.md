## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Estimation Duels]] — how big is $\frac{1}{0.032}$?
   Closest estimate, no calculator, wins
2. Random groups — [[The Reciprocal]] at the boards: from the graph
   of $f$, build the graph of $\frac{1}{f}$. This is the unit's
   diagnostic — unmarked, and what it tells me is how much of Unit 1's
   graph-reading is now automatic
3. Consolidate: [[Rational Functions]] — where $f$ is zero, what must
   $\frac{1}{f}$ do, exactly?
4. The vertical lines your groups drew have a name — it arrives next
   class
5. Journal collection — hand in your Unit 1 volume on the way out
