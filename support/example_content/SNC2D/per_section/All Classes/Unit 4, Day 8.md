## Agenda

1. Warm-up: convex or concave — three lenses, decided by feel and by
   diagram
2. Sort every light source in this room by how the light is actually made:
   the tubes overhead, a phone screen, the window, the exit sign, a glow
   stick. Defend your piles
3. Name it: [[Light in the Room]] — the ways light gets made, and where the
   visible strip sits among the invisible ones on either side of it
4. Then the instruments: the eye, a phone camera, the projector, somebody's
   glasses. Predict what each lens is doing and why that one was chosen

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Journal: the lens you use most, and what it is doing.
- [ ] Find one source of light at home that is still glowing after you
      switch off what was lighting it. Name the mechanism.
