Bad footage is worse than none, because you will trust it — a clip that cuts
off your feet will convince you your landings are fine. Ten minutes of setup
is the whole difference.

## Framing

Film the **whole body, all the time**, including the floor under it and a
metre of air above the head — the feet are the evidence, and they are what an
amateur frame loses first. Hold the phone sideways, in landscape, because a
dancer travels sideways. Do not zoom, follow, or walk closer: a locked camera
shows how much space the movement covered; a moving one hides it.

## Where to put the camera

- **Distance.** Far enough back that you stay in frame at the widest point of
  the phrase, then a metre further. Walk it first and watch the edges.
- **Height.** Hip height, level. A phone on the floor makes everyone look
  monumental and lies about extensions; from above, levels flatten away.
- **Angle.** Square on to the front you dance to — and when checking
  [[Alignment and Placement]], film a second take from the side.
- **Stability.** Something solid — a chair, a ledge, a bag against a wall. A
  held phone shakes even when its owner swears it does not.

## Light, sound, and groups

Put the light in front of you, not behind: filming toward a window turns
everyone into a silhouette, the commonest failure in a studio with one glass
wall. If music matters, play it loud enough for the phone to catch; if not,
film in silence — see [[With Music and Without]]. For a group, one person
films everybody, starting before the entrance and running past the exit.

## Before you press record

A run-through, every time. The boxes below are printed, not stored, so copy
the list into your own notes if you want to keep track.

- [ ] Landscape, whole body, floor visible
- [ ] Camera steady, hip height, square to the front
- [ ] Light in front of you, not behind
- [ ] Storage free, and a phone that is not about to die
- [ ] Say the date and what the clip is, out loud, at the start

## Filing it

Rename the file the same day, always in the same shape:
`unit2-phrase-draft3-jan22`. Put it in the unit folder in
[[The Dance Portfolio]], keep a second copy off your phone, and remember:
[[Video of Yourself]] only works if the first file can still be found.
