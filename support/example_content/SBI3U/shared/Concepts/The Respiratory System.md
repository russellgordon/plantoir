Gas exchange happens by diffusion, which is passive and only works over
short distances. Everything about the respiratory system is arranged to
make those distances short and the area enormous.

## The numbers

About 300 million alveoli, a total surface of roughly 70 m², separated
from blood by two cell layers about 0.5 µm thick. Diffusion across that is
fast; diffusion across a centimetre would not be.

## Breathing is not gas exchange

Ventilation moves air; exchange happens at the alveolus; transport is the
circulatory system's job. Students who keep those three separate answer
these questions correctly, and those who merge them do not.

## Control

Breathing rate is set mainly by carbon dioxide concentration, not oxygen —
which is why holding your breath becomes unbearable long before you are
short of oxygen, and why hyperventilating before a swim is dangerous.
