1. Bench rules before anything else: [[Lab Safety and WHMIS]] — organic
   solvents are flammable and the vapour is the hazard, not the liquid

   [[The Lab Reports]] — today is the first of the four, and the row
   about working safely and effectively is marked from what I see
3. Investigation: [[Boiling Points and Structure]] — measure, do not look up

   how I find out what Grade 11 actually left you with, and it decides
   what Days 2 to 7 are for

6. Finding any of this again: [[Using This Site]], [[Key Links]]
