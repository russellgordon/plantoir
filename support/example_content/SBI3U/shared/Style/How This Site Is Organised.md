Everything in this course lives in one of a few places, and the sidebar
mirrors them exactly.

## The folders

- **[[Concepts/index|Concepts]]** — one page per idea, written once and
  linked from every class that uses it.
- **[[Investigations/index|Investigations]]** — what we do in the lab: the
  question, the safety notes, the procedure, and what to record.
- **[[Exercises/index|Exercises]]** — practice sets with the answers
  folded away.
- **[[Discussions/index|Discussions]]** — questions biology cannot settle
  on its own, where the biology is still the reason the conversation is
  worth having.
- **[[Portfolios/index|Portfolios]]** — your own record of the course.
- **[[Reference/index|Reference]]** — the things you look up: units,
  uncertainty, how to draw a graph that says something.
- **[[Tasks/index|Tasks]]** — anything I mark.
- **Setup** and **Style** — how the course runs, and how this site works.

## Classes

Every class has a page under [[All Classes/index|All Classes]], named for
its unit and day. A class page is an agenda: what we did, in order, with
links to the pages it used, and a short list of things to do before next
time. Nothing is explained on a class page — it links to the page that
explains it.

## Why it is built this way

Because biology repeats itself. Surface area explains the small intestine
in Unit 4 and the root hair in Unit 5; selection explains antibiotic
resistance in Unit 2 and crop breeding in Unit 5. If each explanation
lived inside its lesson you would have several that drift apart. They live
in [[Concepts/index|Concepts]] instead, and the lessons link to them.
