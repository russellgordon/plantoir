## Agenda

1. Twenty minutes on the weakest row you named last class — nothing
   else, however tempting
2. Hand in [[The Green Audit]] — day 4, with ten minutes first for the
   milestone entry in your [[Code Journal]]: which number surprised
   you, and what you assumed before you measured. Your own mark for
   the audit draws on that entry, so it is written in this room
3. Retrieval clinic: one question each from [[Loops Practice]],
   [[Lists Practice]] and [[Text Practice]] — chosen by you for being
   the one you would rather avoid
4. Then a short checkpoint, on your own: read a program, find a fault,
   write a short one from a specification
5. Mark your own against the posted solution; write what you would
   revise — this one is for you, not for me
