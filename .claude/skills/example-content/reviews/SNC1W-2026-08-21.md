### 2. **APPLIED** — `Unit 2 Test`: Cross-strand non-example in success criteria table
- **Files:** `shared/Tasks/Unit 2 Test.md:33`.
- **Finding:** In the success criteria table for the Unit 2 Test (Biology / Sustainable Ecosystems and Climate Change), the illustrative non-example under "Explaining rather than naming" read `"It is a chemical change" earns little; the evidence that makes it one earns the marks.` Chemical change is Unit 3 (Chemistry), not Unit 2.
- **Fix:** Replaced with an authentic ecological mechanism: `| Explaining rather than naming | "It disrupts the ecosystem" earns little; the mechanism (e.g. bioaccumulation, altered nitrogen cycling, or lost habitat) that disrupts it earns the marks. The same is true of every part of this paper |`.
