## Across the course

- [ ] [[Your First Entry]], untouched
- [ ] At least twenty-five journal entries, dated
- [ ] Annotated copies of the short stories and of two acts of the play
- [ ] Passage notes for your seminar novel, with page numbers
- [ ] Your vocabulary page

## The assessed pieces, with drafts

- [ ] [[The Story Behind the Story]] — story, craft note, and draft
- [ ] [[The Scene, Staged]] — script with blocking, and the director's
      note
- [ ] [[The Literary Essay]] — outline, draft with feedback, final
- [ ] [[The Seminar]] — your question, your passages, your follow-up
- [ ] [[The Media Deconstruction]] — the analysis and the paired texts
      you made

## For the examination

- [ ] Your essay outline template, the one that works for you
- [ ] The list of texts, with two passages each you could write about
      cold
- [ ] [[Showing Growth]] — the pair, and three named differences

> [!warning] The one people forget
> The drafts. Nothing in this folder carries a mark — but a final copy
> with nothing behind it leaves you nothing to set beside it in
> [[Showing Growth]], and that pair is the only place you get to see how
> far the term actually moved you.
