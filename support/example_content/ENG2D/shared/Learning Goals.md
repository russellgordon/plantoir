Grade 10 academic English is where the work stops being about whether
you understood and starts being about whether you can prove it. By
January you will be able to:

**Read a text you have never seen and build an argument about it** —
with evidence chosen rather than found, and an objection answered.

**Write an essay that a reader follows without effort**, because the
paragraphs are ordered and the sentences are made rather than assembled.

**Hold your own in a seminar** — the kind where you have to have read the
thing, and where somebody will disagree with you in front of everybody.

**Take a media text apart and say what it is doing to its audience**,
including the ones you enjoy.

**Write the literacy test without being surprised by it**, because you
will have practised its forms deliberately.
