Nothing here is expensive, and nothing here is optional-in-name-only — bring
these and class runs the way the agenda says it will.

## Every day

- Something to write with and something to write on. Paper is fine; so is a
  laptop, if you actually use it for notes and not for everything else.
- Whatever we are reading that week. If it is a shared class text, it stays
  in the room; if it is your own copy, it travels with you.
- Your reading journal (see [[Your Reading Life]]) — most entries are
  written in class, so it needs to be with you, not at home.

## During each unit's task

- Unit 1 and Unit 2: nothing beyond the above — both essays are drafted and
  revised on whatever you are already writing with.
- Unit 3: comfortable clothes on rehearsal and performance days (Days
  57–61). Nobody needs a costume; nobody should be worried about one.
- Unit 4: if your Independent Culminating Study is a podcast or video essay,
  a phone or laptop that can record audio is enough — the school has a
  small number of headset microphones you can sign out for production days
  (Days 79–80), first come, first served.

## What you do not need to bring

A printed copy of every text we discuss — class sets are provided for the
shared readings. And nothing digital needs to be purchased: every tool this
course actually requires is either free or already provided.

If bringing something on this list is ever a genuine problem — cost,
access, anything — say so before it becomes a reason you fall behind. See
[[Getting Help]].
