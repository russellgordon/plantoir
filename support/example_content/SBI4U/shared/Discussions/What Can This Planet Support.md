Carrying capacity is a well-defined idea for yeast in a flask. For humans
it is well defined only once you say what standard of living you mean.

## The biology you need first

[[Population Growth]] and
[[Human Population and Sustainability]] — including the demographic
transition, and the difference between population size and per-person
footprint.

## The question

Estimates of Earth's human carrying capacity range from under two billion
to over forty. Why do serious researchers disagree by a factor of twenty,
and what assumption is doing most of the work?

## Rules for this one

Any number brought to this discussion comes with its assumptions
attached — diet, energy source, and standard of living. A number without
those is not an estimate, it is a slogan.
