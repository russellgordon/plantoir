## CONFIRMED

### 1. **APPLIED** — The pass fixed milestone-entry wording on three task pages and missed the fourth
`shared/Tasks/The Sound of Two Waves.md:51-56` still reads "The **[[Math Journal]] entry** on where the stopwatch and the model disagreed completes the evidence", and line 65 adds a criteria row "| An entry that names the disagreement | **The milestone entry** … |". **Fix:** adopt the Rollercoaster's wording, adapted to this task.

### 2. **APPLIED** — Same page: no individually-evaluated element on a paired task, and no self-assessment
The pass changed the at-a-glance to "Pairs, **marked as individuals**". `How Marks Work.md:108-112` promises "Every task page that puts you with somebody else names the piece that is evaluated as yours alone — the constraint paragraphs with your initials beside them, **the half of the proof you wrote**, the milestone entry, and what you say when I ask you about it." "The half of the proof you wrote" appears nowhere on The Sound of Two Waves; "the constraint paragraphs with your initials beside them" appears nowhere on The Rollercoaster, whose deliverable is "a paragraph per constraint" with no author attached. Two of four paired tasks hand in one shared artefact.

**Fix:** add the individual-element paragraph to both, and the `Judging Your Own Work` paragraph to The Sound of Two Waves — `Unit 3, Day 17.md` item 2 already schedules the ten minutes.

### 3. **APPLIED** — A criteria row that obeying the schedule makes impossible, on evidence with no class period
`The Sound of Two Waves.md:62` — "| Ears first | The throb timed **before the algebra** explains it |". But `Unit 3, Day 16.md` item 2 is the working period for the algebra, and that day's homework is "Prediction made; **recording taken**." So the schedule puts the algebra in class and the recording at home, in the reverse order, and the Day 16 conference asks whether the prediction matches a recording not yet due. Three defects: a row every student fails by obeying the schedule; evaluated evidence produced unsupervised; a conference that cannot happen.

**Fix:** put the listening and timing into the first task period; drop "recording taken" from Day 16's homework.

### 4. **APPLIED** — Two forward references on `Unit 1, Day 16.md` are wrong by three classes

## What was done with the SUSPECTED findings, 2026-08-20

**14 — applied.** It was a real hole, not a wording gap. The task now has both
missing legs: a milestone ("One data point set aside BEFORE fitting, then used
to test the finished model") and a criteria row ("A model that was tested").

**15 — applied by adding contents, not by dropping codes.** The examination now
asks for a ratio evaluated with a calculator from a decimal radian measure
(B1.3) and a sinusoid whose equation is written from its graph (B2.6). Dropping
the codes was the other option and was rejected: the examination is the only
assessed page for both, so dropping them would have left two expectations with
no assessed home at all — trading a contents gap for a coverage-map lie.

**16 — half done, and the half left undone is the interesting one.** C3.7
("applications of polynomial and simple rational functions") had NO assessed
home anywhere in the payload, and Safe Concentration's rows "Exact edges" and
"Equation against inequality" are exactly what it describes, so it was added and
passes the three-legged test.

C2.1 was NOT removed, and the reviewer is right that it is a poor fit —
C(t) = at/(t² + b) is not the reciprocal of a linear or quadratic function.
It stays because Safe Concentration is its ONLY assessed home: the other three
pages carrying it are Concepts, Explorations and Exercises. Removing it would
strand C2.1 exactly as dropping B1.3 would have stranded B1.3. Resolving it
properly means deciding where in the course C2.1 SHOULD be assessed, which is an
arc decision rather than a conformance one. Recorded rather than papered over.

**17 — applied.** Day 9 asked students to "shade your window on the graph and
defend both of its edges" one class before Day 10 fitted the model that defines
those edges. Day 9 now asks for the band marked across the plotted data with
each edge estimated; Day 10 says the edges are "solved, not estimated".

**18 — applied.** The mark page rests the mathematical-process exception on four
things and only two had a criteria row. Both pages already made the point
central in prose, so the row was a missing leg rather than a new demand: The
Rollercoaster gained "A miss you measured" and The Sound of Two Waves gained
"A gap you found first".

**19 — applied.** The Day 15 probe was the same move as the Day 12 one — a
dismissal of the family choice, refuted — with the rehearsal coming first. It is
now a steelman: "Make the strongest case you can FOR the family you rejected —
the best version of it, not a straw one. Then tell me what kills it." Still
D3.1, different cognitive move, and the block now says WHY it differs so the
next editor does not helpfully converge them again.

**20 — the countable ones applied.** "one half … the other and larger half"
became "the smaller of the two pieces". "Runs across six periods" became four,
which is what the class pages give.
