A page a week, in your own words, written for one reader: you at the end of
the course, trying to remember why any of this made sense.

## What goes in an entry

1. **The idea, in your own words.** Not the definition from the site —
   yours. If they match word for word, you copied rather than understood.
2. **One example or diagram** you chose, labelled by you.
3. **One thing that is still murky**, stated as a question.

Three quarters of a page is plenty. Consistency beats volume.

## Why in your own words

Biology has more vocabulary than any other science you will take, and
vocabulary is where understanding hides. The moment you try to explain
meiosis without using the word "meiosis", you find out whether you have
it.

[[Portfolio Checklist]] is what a complete portfolio holds by the end of the
course.

> [!tip] Drawing counts
> A labelled diagram you drew yourself is worth three paragraphs copied
> from anywhere. Draw badly and label carefully.
