Homeostasis is dynamic: variables oscillate around a set point rather than
sitting still, and the oscillation is the evidence that control is working.

## The loop

Stimulus → receptor → control centre → effector → response → the stimulus
is reduced. Negative feedback, and it is the structure of nearly every
regulatory system in the body.

## Thermoregulation

Hypothalamus as control centre. Too warm: vasodilation, sweating,
behaviour. Too cold: vasoconstriction, shivering, piloerection, behaviour.
Fever is the set point being raised deliberately, which is why you feel
cold while running a temperature.

## Blood glucose

Insulin lowers it, glucagon raises it, both from the pancreas. Type 1
diabetes is loss of the insulin-producing cells; type 2 is cells becoming
resistant to insulin's signal. Same variable, two different failures in
the loop, which is why the treatments differ.

## Positive feedback

Amplifies instead of damping, and always ends in a definite event:
childbirth, blood clotting, the action potential. A loop with no endpoint
is a system out of control.
