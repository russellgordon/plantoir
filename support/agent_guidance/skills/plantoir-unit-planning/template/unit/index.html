How the sections differ

  • Section 1 meets on Monday and loses the Wednesday of the first week, so it writes the test on Thursday and has the showcase on the last Friday.
  • Section 2 does not meet on Tuesdays, so the review falls on Thursday and the test on Friday. It has no showcase day.
  • A colleague's section starts a day later and writes the quiz after the long weekend, on Tuesday February 16.

One card per lesson, in the order the calendar uses them. The rows of each card follow the lesson shape on the teacher's How I Teach page; the labels below are examples.

L1 Signs of a chemical change✓ reviewed

Textbook 5.1
Warm-up

Four photographs (rust, a burnt match, melting ice, a fizzing tablet): which are new substances?

Lesson

The five signs of a chemical change, each shown at the front, and why one sign alone is not proof.

Practice

Sorting cards in groups, then a short written check.
