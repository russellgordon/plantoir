Questions with no answer key.

**None of these is marked.** What is worth having is the argument itself:
evidence used, weaknesses acknowledged, and whether you engaged the strongest
version of the other view rather than the easiest one. Those are the habits the
marked tasks then ask you for in writing, which is where they count.

> [!note] You may change your mind
> Out loud, mid-discussion. That is the best possible outcome and I will say so.
