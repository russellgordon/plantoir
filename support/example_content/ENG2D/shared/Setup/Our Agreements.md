Half of this course is argument. These are the agreements that make
argument productive rather than merely loud.

## What we agree to

1. **Attack the idea, never the person.** "That reading ignores the last
   act" is analysis. "You never do the reading" ends the discussion for
   everybody.
2. **Bring the text.** A claim with a page number behind it beats a
   confident one without.
3. **Steelman before you object.** Say the strongest version of the other
   position before you take it apart. It is the single most useful habit
   in this room, and it will still be useful in twenty years.
4. **You may pass**, and nobody will ask why.
5. **Changing your mind out loud is the best thing that happens here**,
   and it will always be treated that way.
6. **What is said about somebody's own life stays here.**

## The texts, and what they deal with

This course includes a play about a feud that kills children, a novel
about a community cut off during a collapse, short fiction about violence
and grief, and media that is designed to move you without your noticing.
Every text page names what it deals with at the top.

If you need to step out of a class, step out. You do not need to explain,
and it costs you nothing. Come and see me afterwards and we will find
another way in.

> [!important] Reading Indigenous writing
> [[Moon of the Crusted Snow]] is by Waubgeshig Rice, who is Anishinaabe
> from Wasauksing First Nation, and the novel is set in an Anishinaabe
> community. We name the nation, we use the words the writer uses, and we
> do not decide on a community's behalf what its story is "really"
> about — the reasoning is on [[Whose Reading Counts]].

## Using AI honestly

A language model can produce a paragraph about *Romeo and Juliet* in four
seconds. It cannot read for you, and every assessment in this course
measures reading.

| Fine | Not fine |
| --- | --- |
| Asking what a Shakespearean word meant in 1595 | Asking what a scene means |
| Asking it to quiz you before a seminar | Asking it to generate your seminar questions |
| Asking for a synonym you are reaching for | Asking it to rewrite your paragraph |

Where you use it, say so and say how, in a line at the end. That is the
rule professionals work under and it costs you nothing.
