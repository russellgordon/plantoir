> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** half a class. **Pairs.** A distribution, not a single number.

A dropped ruler, caught between finger and thumb, measures the whole
reflex arc: retina to brain to muscle.

## Method

1. Ten catches per person, discarding none. The distances convert to times
   with $t = \sqrt{2d/g}$.
2. Repeat under one changed condition per pair: with warning, distracted,
   with the non-dominant hand, or after exercise.
3. Pool the class data for both conditions.

## Analysis

1. Report the mean and the SPREAD, not just the mean. Biology varies, and
   ten trials shows you by how much.
2. Did your changed condition shift the mean beyond the spread? If not,
   you cannot claim an effect — say so.
3. Trace the pathway the signal took, naming the structures from
   [[The Nervous System]].
4. A true spinal reflex is faster than this. Why — what does this task
   include that a knee-jerk does not?
