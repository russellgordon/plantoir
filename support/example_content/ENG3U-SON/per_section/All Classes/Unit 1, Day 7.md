## Agenda

1. Workshop: [[Building a Thesis Statement]] — topic, to question, to a
   claim someone could actually disagree with
2. First look at [[Judging Your Own Work]] — modelled together, out loud,
   on an anonymized sample paragraph, not anyone's own writing yet. Watch
   for what a real self-check sounds like, and what it isn't.
3. Practice on your own: draft one thesis statement about a story we've
   read so far, then test it against the "could a reasonable reader
   disagree" question
