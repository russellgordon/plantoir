## Section __SECTION_NUMBER__ — notes to myself

- **The culminating task needs a local decision calendar, and it needs
  it before Unit 3 starts.** [[The Civic Action Project]] only works if
  each group can find a real decision point with a real date — a council
  or committee agenda, a board meeting, a consultation closing date, a
  bill at committee, a budget deputation window. Build that list in
  Early on: municipal and school-board agendas for the months ahead,
  any provincial or federal consultations still open, and the dates
  deputations must be requested by. Groups that cannot find a date end up
  writing to nobody in particular, and the mark suffers for a reason that
  was never theirs.
- **[[Indigenous Governance]] needs your own treaty area named**, and
  where it is possible, that nation's or those nations' own published
  material rather than a summary written about them. The page names
  specific nations only where the facts were verified; the local
  particulars are yours to supply, and students notice immediately when
  they are missing. Check the wording with whoever in your board holds
  that file before the class rather than after.
- **[[Our Working Agreement]] promises that no mark depends on a
  student's politics.** That promise is load-bearing — it is what lets a
  class argue about housing, policing, or immigration in front of each
  other — and it is only worth anything if the marking backs it up. Read
  it before the first class, decide you can keep it, and then keep it
  visibly: mark reasoning and evidence, say out loud when a well-argued
  position you disagree with earns full marks, and do not let a seminar
  turn into a count of who agrees with you.
- Book the room with a projector for the [[How Government Works]]
  walkthroughs in Unit 1.

## The arithmetic and the calendar

Forty-two periods of 75 minutes plus a two-and-a-half-hour final
evaluation is 55.0 hours — one half credit exactly. Units run 14, 14, and
14. The manifest sets `class_start_school_day` to 43, so this course's
first class falls on the school day after Career Studies' last one.

The winter break lands inside Unit 3. Whatever the arc says, the two
periods either side of it are the ones that go missing, so put the
checkpoint — period 5 of the culminating task — before the break rather
than after it if your calendar allows.

## Where the coverage is thin

Every specific expectation is addressed. Fourteen are addressed exactly
once, and most of those are the narrower B2 governance expectations,
where one page really does the work and a second transclusion would be
decoration. If you want any of them met twice, add a source or an
investigation that genuinely teaches it.
