SCORING
  A call cut off at the cap (truncated arguments, or finish_reason 'length')
  counts as a MISS whatever tool it names - the suites score it by name, and
  the app refuses it (wording.answerWasCutOff). Recounted from each run's
  per-trial rows.
  "The changed tools' probes" = every probe whose acceptable answer includes
  check_section, add_next_class or read_remembered_timetable, plus every
  probe whose BEFORE or AFTER answer names one of them.

PASS CRITERIA (bundle 9 ruling P2 binds, and is stricter than #352's per-tool veto)
  The descriptions MOVE only if ALL of these hold, across S1, S2 and S3:
   (a) no polarity inversion in AFTER that BEFORE lacks;
   (b) no changed tool loses 3 or more trials net across its probes;
   (c) neither suite's model-seen total falls by more than 5 points;
   (d) no new cut-offs in AFTER.
  If any fails: NO description moves (not only the failing tool's), and #352
  closes with this measurement as the reason. If the measurement cannot be
  completed: nothing moves and #352 stays open.
  If all hold: Windows' served descriptions become the contract text for
  every shared tool except publish_pages and unpublish_pages; NarrowToLocal
  stops calling Briefly(); narrow-tools.py and NarrowToolsMirrorTests follow.
