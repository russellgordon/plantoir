Grade 12 university English is the last English course most people take.
By the end of the course you will be able to:

**Read a text through more than one lens, and say what each one shows and
costs.** Not "apply a theory" — use a critical approach the way a tool is
used, because it makes something visible.

**Sustain an argument across two thousand words** without repeating
yourself, and know why your paragraphs are in the order they are in.

**Compare two texts with one argument that needs both**, rather than
writing two essays and stapling them.

**Defend your reading out loud**, to people who have read the same books
and disagree.

**Read your own prose as a text**: for structure, for diction, for the
sentence doing the work.
