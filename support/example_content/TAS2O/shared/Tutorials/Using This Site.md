This site is the newsroom's memory — every class, concept, task, and
practice set lives here. Five habits make it fast to use.

## The five habits

1. **Land on today.** The home page always surfaces the most recent
   class, so "what did we cover?" and "what is due?" are one click
   from anywhere. Out on a shoot or away sick? Start there before
   asking anyone.
2. **Search first.** Press `Cmd + K` or `Ctrl + K` — whichever your
   keyboard offers — and type any word: "caption", "consent",
   "deadline". Search reads every page at once; it is nearly always
   faster than navigating.
3. **Hover before you click.** Rest your pointer on any
   [[News Values|link like this one]] and a preview of the target
   page pops up. You can often confirm a definition without ever
   leaving the story you are reading.
4. **Read the backlinks.** At the bottom of each page, *Backlinks*
   lists every page that links **to** this one. On a concept page,
   that is an instant map of where the idea gets used — the site's
   web, walked backwards.
5. **Predict before you unfold.** Practice pages in Exercises fold
   their answers away — click the callout and it expands. The fold
   is a tool, not a lock: commit to your own headline or caption
   first, then open it. Peeking first feels efficient and teaches
   nothing.
