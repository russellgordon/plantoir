**1.** A $0.20\ \text{kg}$ ball on a $0.75\ \text{m}$ string swings in
a horizontal circle, completing 20 revolutions in $12\ \text{s}$. Find
the speed and the centripetal force.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> $T = 0.60\ \text{s}$, $v = 2\pi r/T = 7.9\ \text{m/s}$,
> $F_c = mv^2/r = 16.6\ \text{N}$.

**2.** A car of mass $1400\ \text{kg}$ rounds a curve of radius
$55\ \text{m}$ at $18\ \text{m/s}$. What minimum coefficient of static
friction is required?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> $F_c = mv^2/r = 8250\ \text{N}$; $\mu_s \ge F_c/(mg) = 0.60$. Wet
> asphalt is around 0.4, which is why that corner has a warning sign.

**3.** A conical pendulum hangs at $25^\circ$ from the vertical with a
radius of $0.40\ \text{m}$. Find the speed of the bob.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> $v = \sqrt{rg\tan\theta} = \sqrt{0.40 \times 9.8 \times 0.466} = 1.35\ \text{m/s}$.
> Mass never appears — as in [[The Flying Pig]].

**4.** What is the minimum speed at the top of a vertical loop of radius
$8.0\ \text{m}$ for a cart to maintain contact?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> $v = \sqrt{gr} = 8.9\ \text{m/s}$. Below that, the track would need to
> pull the cart inward, which a track cannot do.

**5.** A clinical laboratory centrifuge spins blood test vials at
$3600\ \text{rpm}$ with an effective sample radius of $12\ \text{cm}$.
(a) Determine the period $T$, frequency $f$, and centripetal acceleration
$a_c$ in $\text{m/s}^2$ and in multiples of $g$. (b) Assess the societal
and environmental impact of centrifuge technology in healthcare diagnostics
and industrial environmental wastewater treatment.

> [!success]- Answer 5
> (a) Frequency $f = 3600\ \text{rev} / 60\ \text{s} = 60\ \text{Hz}$;
> period $T = 1/f = 0.0167\ \text{s}$. Speed
> $v = 2\pi r f = 2\pi(0.12)(60) = 45.2\ \text{m/s}$. Centripetal
> acceleration $a_c = v^2/r = (45.2)^2 / 0.12 = 1.71 \times 10^4\ \text{m/s}^2 \approx 1740g$.
> (b) Centrifuges rapidly separate blood plasma from cellular components,
> enabling early disease diagnosis, blood transfusions, and rapid chemical
> assays that save lives daily. In environmental management, industrial
> dewatering centrifuges remove toxic particulate sludge from municipal
> wastewater and industrial effluents, protecting watersheds while balancing
> electrical energy consumption.

**6.** A $65\ \text{kg}$ passenger stands on a digital scale in a
high-rise elevator. (a) Draw a free-body diagram and calculate the scale
reading (apparent weight $F_N$) when the elevator accelerates upward at
$1.8\ \text{m/s}^2$, travels upward at a constant $8.0\ \text{m/s}$, and
decelerates upward at $2.2\ \text{m/s}^2$. (b) Assess the impact of traction
elevator systems on urban land use and carbon footprint.

> [!success]- Answer 6
> (a) Upward acceleration: $F_{\text{net}} = F_N - mg = ma \implies F_N = m(g + a) = 65(9.8 + 1.8) = 754\ \text{N}$.
> Constant speed ($a = 0$): $F_N = mg = 65(9.8) = 637\ \text{N}$.
> Deceleration ($a = -2.2\ \text{m/s}^2$): $F_N = m(g + a) = 65(9.8 - 2.2) = 494\ \text{N}$.
> (b) Counterweighted traction elevators make high-density vertical cities
> possible, concentrating housing, transit, and commerce, which dramatically
> reduces per-capita carbon emissions, road infrastructure costs, and
> agricultural land loss compared to horizontal suburban sprawl.
