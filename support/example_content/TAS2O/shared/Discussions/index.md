A newsroom that never argues is not calm — it is asleep. Every page
is a stack of choices: this story and not that one, this photo, this
headline. Choices have reasons, and reasons can be examined, so this
room argues on purpose, with the coverage itself as the evidence.

| Discussion | The argument |
| --- | --- |
| [[Whose Story Is It]] | Who gets covered — and who is only mentioned |
| [[When Is a Photo True]] | Edits that serve truth, edits that invent it |
| [[Free Press, School Press]] | What a school paper can and should say |
| [[The Comment Section]] | What happens after publish — and to whom |

The ground rules come from [[Our Newsroom Standards]]: hard on the
work, never on the person — and arrive ready to change your mind,
because an editor who cannot be argued with was never deciding.
