When two waves arrive at the same place, their displacements add. That is
the whole principle, and everything else in this page follows from it.

## Constructive and destructive

Crest on crest gives a bigger crest: **constructive** interference. Crest
on trough cancels: **destructive**. Two speakers playing the same tone
produce a room with loud spots and quiet spots that you can walk between —
the quiet ones are not places the sound failed to reach, they are places
where two sounds arrive out of step.

## Beats

Two frequencies close together produce a slow throb — the **beat
frequency**:

$$f_{beat} = |f_1 - f_2|$$

Two tuning forks at 256 Hz and 260 Hz give four beats per second. Tune one
until the beats slow to nothing and the two match exactly. Every guitarist
uses this, and it is a more sensitive comparison than any ear alone.

## Noise cancellation

Headphones that cancel noise are running destructive interference on
purpose: a microphone hears the incoming wave and the speaker plays its
inverse. It works best on steady low sounds — engine drone — and worst on
sudden ones, because the electronics need a moment to catch up.
