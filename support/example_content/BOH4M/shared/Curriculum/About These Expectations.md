> [!warning] The codes on these pages are not printed in the document
> The 2006 Business Studies curriculum prints no codes: strands are
> titled headings, overall expectations are unnumbered bullets, and
> specific expectations are unnumbered dashes under italic sub-headings.
> The codes used here — `A1`, `C2.4`, `E3.6` — are **positional**,
> assigned the way boards and departments commonly do it: strands
> lettered A to E in document order, each strand's overall expectations
> numbered in printed order, and each specific expectation numbered
> within its group.
>
> The pairing of a sub-heading to an overall expectation is the
> document's own, not an inference: every strand prints the same number
> of sub-headings as overall expectations, in the same order. So `A2` is
> both "the second overall expectation" and "the Business Communication
> group", and `A2.3` is the third dash beneath it. If your department
> numbers them differently, the wording is what matters — it is
> untouched.

Each page holds one expectation, word for word, and nothing else. Every
lesson, case, simulation, and task in this course links back to the codes
it addresses, and the codes are tags, so every page tagged `#C2` can be
gathered in one place.

## Why the codes matter more than they look

A code is a shared address. When a student asks "why are we doing this?",
the answer is a link. When a department writes a course outline, or a
teacher takes over a class partway through, the codes are what make one
person's planning legible to another.

## How this course reads them

The overall expectations (`A1`, `D4`, …) name what the course is for; the
specific expectations (`A1.1`, `D4.3`, …) name what we practise week to
week. The five strands are the four managerial functions — leading,
planning, organizing, controlling — with the foundations they rest on.

One further note on the wording: the document prints "opportunties" in
`D3.3`. That is the Ministry's own typographical error, and it is
reproduced here rather than silently corrected, because these pages are
meant to be the document.
