PASS RULE (all must hold, on EACH tier; one local list, so a failure on
either tier is the fallback for both)
  (a) the arms' local tool lists differ by exactly the one appended tool
      (parsed), and scripts/test_tool_surface_digest.py passes on the branch
      with the new digest.
  (b) NO REGRESSION: on every M1a-M1d probe, AFTER's correct count is at
      least min(BEFORE, BEFORE'). A probe at 10/10 in both BEFORE arms must
      stay 10/10. For M1d a row's count is its trials that reached
      add_next_class or were declined with no unit/days key (a `pointed`
      row) or that reached add_next_class (a control).
      WHY min(BEFORE, BEFORE') and not #440's "identical": #440's surface
      was byte-equal before and after, so ANY move was a harness or arm
      fault. Here the surface differs by one schema, so the prompt differs
      and a greedy run may legitimately move inside this Mac's own
      run-to-run band; BEFORE' measures that band on the identical surface
      the same evening, and the rule forbids AFTER falling below the lower
      of the two. It is not a tolerance: a probe that never moved in the band
      may not move at all.
  (c) NO THEFT: no M1 probe and no M2 nearMiss sends open_in_obsidian in any
      trial. And every M2 nearMiss keeps the tool it gets without the verb:
      its count for the tool (or decline) chosen most often across BEFORE and
      BEFORE' is at least min(BEFORE, BEFORE') on AFTER.
  (d) VETO: zero polarity inversions in every suite, every arm.
  (e) every M2 `accepted` sentence reaches open_in_obsidian, a decline, or
      one of the four reads (list_pages, read_page, check_section,
      read_remembered_timetable) in 10/10 on AFTER - never any other tool
      (rebuild_preview included: it is a write on the surface's own readOnly
      flag). On the LARGER tier the accepted set reaches open_in_obsidian in
      at least 90% of its trials (63 of 70).
  Recorded, deciding nothing: prompt tokens before and after (one more
  schema), median ms, completion tokens; the recordedOnly sentence ("Open
  Unit 2, Day 3 in Obsidian", which names a page the tool cannot take).
