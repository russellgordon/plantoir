## Agenda

1. [[The Optics Design]] submitted at the start of class: the photograph,
   the prediction documents with the measured column filled in, the test
   report, the disagreement, the comparison and the spectrum section. The
   device itself stays with you — you need it tomorrow
2. Then thirty-five minutes: [[The Science Showcase]] working period —
   the career and the scientist researched and written here, on one page,
   handed in at the start of next class
3. Then choose the earlier investigation you will present, and what you
   will say about it
4. Retrieval clinic: a cell diagram, a balanced equation, a climate graph,
   and an image predicted cold — one from each unit, no notes
