## Agenda

1. Revisit [[Boys and Girls]]: whose eyes are we looking through, and how
   far back does the telling stand from the events it describes?
2. Name the concept: [[Point of View and Narrative Distance]]
3. On your own, then in pairs: find one "seam" moment in the text — a place
   where the grown narrator briefly steps forward to tell you something the
   child living through the scene couldn't have — and explain what added
   distance it reveals
