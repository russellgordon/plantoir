## Agenda

1. Standup — crews choose today, and crew 1 prints before the bell
2. Crew meeting: compare the candidates against the brief's criteria
   and choose one to print — the reason goes in writing
3. Slice it; write down the slicer's time and grams. Over thirty
   minutes or fifteen grams? Back to the model
4. Crew 1's first version starts printing as soon as it is sliced
   (30 printer minutes)
5. While it prints — the queue as project management:
   [[The Production Pipeline]]; plan backwards from Day 21, and book
   your slot on the board
