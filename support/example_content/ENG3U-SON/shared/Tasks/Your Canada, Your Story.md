Your Independent Culminating Study — the piece the whole course has been
building toward. Choose a Canadian author or a theme running through this
semester's texts (the teacher-approved list favours Canadian and Indigenous
voices — Wagamese, Taylor, and others like them are all fair game, and so
is a theme like "what a story owes the truth" that runs across several
authors). Produce two things:

1. **An analytical piece** — a written argument about your topic, in
   whatever form suits it (essay, extended review, script for a spoken
   piece — your choice, justified).
2. **A media artifact** — a podcast, video essay, or another multimodal
   form that applies what Unit 4 has taught about persuasion and media
   convention to your own topic.

This is the last new work of the course. What follows it is review and the
final evaluation — nothing else gets taught after Day 83.

## Success criteria

| Quality | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| A real argument, not a report | Your analytical piece takes a position on your topic, not just a summary of it |
| Sources genuinely used | Research actually shapes what you argue, cited properly — see [[MLA In-Text Citation]] |
| The media form fits the purpose | Your choice of podcast, video, or another form is the RIGHT one for this topic and audience, and you can say why |
| Convention used on purpose | Whatever techniques your media artifact uses (pacing, visuals, structure — see [[Storyboarding Your Video or Audio Essay]]) are deliberate, not decorative |
| A finished, polished submission | Meets the criteria above by the final hand-in, not just the draft |

## How to work

1. **Day 72 — proposal.** Pitch your topic and form; success criteria
   shared in full.
2. **Day 73 — proposal checkpoint.** I confer with everyone on their pitch.
3. **Day 74 — revise your pitch** based on Day 73's feedback; finalize
   topic and form.
4. **Days 75-76 — research**, including [[From Notes to Outline]] on Day
   76.
5. **Days 77-78 — draft the analytical piece.**
   [[Storyboarding Your Video or Audio Essay]] on Day 78 starts your media plan.
6. **Days 79-80 — production** on your media artifact.
7. **Day 81 — peer feedback and self-assessment.** Structured peer
   feedback, plus [[Judging Your Own Work]] on your own — your fourth
   independent use this course.
8. **Day 82 — revise** based on Day 81's feedback.
9. **Day 83 — final polish and submission.**

## The Reflective Commentary

A short written piece (300-400 words, part of your submission, marked
separately from the analytical piece and the media artifact) that answers:
which of your two pieces — the writing or the media artifact — do you think
shows your strongest work this course, and why? What decision from your
production process (a script cut, a structural choice, a source you
rejected) are you most confident was the right one? This is evaluated on
the specificity and honesty of your answer, the same standard every
Reflective Commentary in this course has used since [[Judging Your Own Work]]
was introduced in Unit 1 — not on how the semester felt.
