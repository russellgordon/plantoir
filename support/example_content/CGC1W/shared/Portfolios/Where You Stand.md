The last entry, written in the final week. One Canadian geographic issue,
and an honest account of how your view of it moved between the start of the
course and now — with the evidence that moved it.

## Choosing the issue

It has to be one you held a position on early: land and treaty rights,
where and whether to build, who pays for climate damage, what a community
owes people arriving from elsewhere, where food should come from. If you
cannot recall an early view, take what you argued about hardest.

## Before you write

Re-read your earliest notes, your [[The Field Notebook\|field notebook]]
entries from the shoreline and the woodlot, all three versions of
[[A Map of Your Own]], and your seminar contributions. Look for the
moment the position shifted, not for a summary of what you now believe.

## The entry (about 700 words)

**What you thought at the start of the course, stated plainly.** Not the polished
version — the actual one, including the part you would now argue against.

**What changed it.** Name the specific thing: a number you looked up
yourself, something you saw standing on the ground, a person's account,
or an argument from a classmate that you could not answer.[^nochange]

**Apply the four concepts to the issue as you now understand it.** What
is significant about *where* this happens, what pattern the data shows,
what is interrelated, and whose perspective changes the answer. Reason
with [[The Concepts of Geographic Thinking]]; do not fill four headings.

**What you still do not know.** The fact or the perspective missing from
your position, and where you would go to get it.

[^nochange]: If your view did not change, that is a legitimate finding —
    but only if you can state the strongest evidence against you and say
    why it did not move you. "I was right all along" without that is not
    a reflection.

## What this is for

Issues like these will reach you as ballots, jobs, and municipal notices
rather than assignments. The habit worth keeping is the one practised
here: hold a position, know what it rests on, know what would change it.
