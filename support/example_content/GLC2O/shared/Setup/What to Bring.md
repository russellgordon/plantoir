A short course cannot afford a period spent waiting for people to find
things. The list below is small on purpose, and most of it is once-only.

## Every day

Tick the boxes here as you pack; your browser keeps the ticks on this
computer, and nobody else sees them. Copy the list into your own notes if
you want it everywhere.

- [ ] Somewhere to write that is not loose paper. A notebook or a single
      document you keep adding to.
- [ ] Your device, charged, if you have one. If you do not, say so and
      one will be arranged — this is not a problem and it does not need
      explaining.
- [ ] The thing the class page asked you to bring. There is usually one,
      and the period is built on it.

## Once, in the first two weeks

- [ ] A folder or drive location for your portfolio, made and named,
      that you will still be able to open in Grade 12.
- [ ] Your course history: what you have taken, what you are taking, and
      the marks. Guidance can print it if you are not sure.
- [ ] A login you can actually use for the school's planning tools,
      including your Individual Pathways Plan.

## For the specific days

Some periods need something you have to bring from outside. They are on
the class pages a week ahead, and they are:

- A real job posting, for [[Reading a Job Ad]].
- The name of one adult who does work you are curious about, for
  [[The Informational Interview]].
- Real numbers — a bill, a receipt, a rent listing, a transit fare — for
  [[The Cost of a Month]].
- Two things you have made or done that you would show someone, for
  [[Your Career Portfolio]].

## What you do not need

No textbook. No calculator beyond the one on your phone. No money for
anything — if a trip or a certification comes up, it is covered, and you
should ask rather than assume otherwise.

## If something is missing

Come anyway. A period you attend without the posting is worth far more
than a period you skip because you did not have it, and there are always
spares.
