  "Five of the 29 probes never reach the model in the app — AssistCardCommand

WHAT CHANGED, 2026-09-19 (issue #202, the mac half of #180)
-----------------------------------------------------------
A model call whose `course` is not this window's is REFUSED before anything
runs — no settling, no plan twin, no approval card, no tool — and the teacher
is told, naming both courses. Rewriting it meant "publish MCV4U's class", typed
in a VVH2O window, publishing a VVH2O class and reporting success. Any
differing code is refused, including one that names no course in the folder,
which gets a second sentence of its own because the first one tells the teacher
to go and open that course.

So for the wrong-course counter specifically, the suite is now LESS strict than
the product rather than more: a response it counts as one fault is, in the app,
a turn that does not run at all.

The suite's own comment was corrected in trimmed-surface-suite.py (its
`app_corrected` list now says "refused by the app" rather than "rewritten by
the app"). No results file was edited.
