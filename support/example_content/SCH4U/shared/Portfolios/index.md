Your own record of how your thinking changed over the semester.

A journal is **chosen, not accumulated**. A handful of entries written
close to the work, while you still remember which model you were leaning
on and why you doubted a reading, beats a folder containing everything
you ever handed in.

You kept one of these last year, so the format will be familiar. What
changed is what an entry has to contain: this year a prediction is
incomplete until it names the model it came from, and a conclusion is
incomplete until it says where that model stopped being trustworthy.

Start with [[Chemistry Journal]] for what this is and why we bother,
then write [[Your First Entry]] in the first week.
[[What a Strong Entry Looks Like]] shows you the standard, and
[[Journal Checklist]] is what I check against at the end.

[[Judging Your Own Work]] is the other habit this folder is for, and it
points outward rather than inward: a routine for reading a draft against
the criteria that will be used on it, while there is still time to
change something. We run it together the first time, in Unit 1.
