Falling behind in an English course rarely happens all at once — it is
usually one missed reading, or one skipped checkpoint, that makes the next
thing harder to do. The earlier you say something, the smaller the fix.

## During class

Put your hand up, or come find me during work time. Every checkpoint day on
a task (see any page in [[Tasks/index|Tasks]]) exists partly so this
conversation can happen before the mark does, not after.

## Outside class

[[Help Sessions]] lists when I am around outside scheduled class time — drop
in with a draft, a question about a reading, or nothing more specific than
"I'm stuck."

## If a reading or a deadline is the problem

Tell me before the due date, not after. A task that has fallen behind
because of something real — illness, a family situation, anything — gets
handled as exactly that; a task that quietly goes missing does not get
noticed until it is much harder to fix.

## If a text itself is the problem

Some of what we read this year is heavy — see [[How We Talk About Hard
Things]]. If a specific text or discussion is genuinely too much for you,
tell me privately. There is always another way to reach the same learning
goal.

## Outside this classroom

The school's student services office and guidance counsellors are a real
resource for anything bigger than one course — workload across your whole
timetable, or anything personal that is affecting your work everywhere, not
just here.
