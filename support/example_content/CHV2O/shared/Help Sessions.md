Times I am in the room and free to work with you. No appointment, no
reason needed.

| When | Where | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday, lunch | Room 118 | Sources you are stuck on, and briefs |
| Thursday, after school until 4:00 | Room 118 | Drafts, and research with the records open |
| Any morning before period 1, by arrangement | Room 118 | Quick questions, marks, deadlines |

This course is ten weeks long and it runs across the winter break, which
means the two weeks either side of it are the ones people lose. Come in
early rather than late.

> [!tip] Bring the document, not a description of it
> The bill, the budget page, the decision itself. Ten minutes with the
> source open beats half an hour of explaining what it seemed to say.

During [[The Civic Action Project]] the Thursday session is for group
meetings — book it, so you are not sharing the room with three other
groups chasing three different deadlines.

If none of these times work, say so and we will find one. See
[[Getting Help]] for the other ways to reach me.
