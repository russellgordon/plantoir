## CONFIRMED

### 1. **APPLIED** — Wholesale Curriculum Text Restoration: 71 Expectation Files Restored Verbatim
- **Files:** All 71 files in `shared/Curriculum/` (12 overall expectations and 59 specific expectations).
- **Source of Truth:** Primary official Ministry curriculum document: *The Ontario Curriculum, Grade 9: English (2023) (ENL1W)*, official PDF extracted from `https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com/fbd574c4-da36-0066-a0c5-849ffb2de96e/23d818cb-21f1-4fa1-bdd6-6f27f5c003fe/English9PDF3.pdf` (pages 39–49).
- **Finding:** Pervasive over-capture and trailing swept heading corruption affected all 71 expectation pages. Because the 2023 Grade 9 English curriculum expectations do not use parenthetical examples ending with `)`, automated scrapers swept the subsequent section or expectation subheadings onto the ends of expectations before the `^text` block anchor (e.g., `A1. Transferable Skills.md` ending with `Receptive and Expressive Communication`, `C2.1.md` ending with `Prereading: Identifying the Purpose for Reading, Listening, and Viewing`, `D1. Developing Ideas and Organizing Content.md` ending with `Purpose and Audience`, and `B3. Language Conventions for Reading and Writing.md` ending with `for Reading and Writing Continuum, Grades 1–9, Overall Expectation B3. Syntax and Sentence Structure`).
- **Fix:** Regenerated and restored all 71 files verbatim with clean, exact Ministry text ending cleanly with ` ^text` block anchors and standard frontmatter (`h3` for overalls, `h4` for specifics).
