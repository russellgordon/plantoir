This page lists what a Guidance and careers website made with Plantoir can show. Each item names a page of the ready-made course where you can see it, and the Help page that says how it is done.

## The ready-made Career Studies course

One code has ready-made pages: **GLC2O**, Career Studies, Grade 10. It is a half credit.

To get it, choose {{menu:newCourse}}, type GLC2O and leave {{control:newCourseSheetPrePopulateWithExampleContent}} turned on. [Make a new course](page:new-course) has the steps. It comes with its folders, its shared pages, and 42 class pages in three units for every section. The class pages are published and dated across the semester, so the first deploy may ask about classes dated later. See [Classes dated after the next class, at Deploy](page:later-classes).

To read it without teaching from it, put it on the Reference Courses shelf. See [Bring in last year's courses](page:import-for-reference).

Any other Guidance and Career Education code offers a starting point for the subject instead. Its folders are Concepts, Activities, Reflections, Portfolios, Tasks, Setup, Style, Tutorials and Curriculum.

## What the pages show

GLC2O is the only ready-made course whose sections get a {{control:myProgressTitle}} page. Its task pages are the reason.

Each of the seven pages in Tasks closes with the heading Success criteria. Under the heading are five or six tick boxes, each worded about the student's own work. This is the heading on the page The Budget, with one of its boxes:

```text wrap
### Success criteria

- [ ] Every figure in my budget has its source and the date I looked.
```

{{picture:subject-guidance-and-careers-1}}
alt: The success criteria at the end of the page The Budget, on the class website, with a box for each criterion.

A section's website gains a {{control:myProgressTitle}} page when a page students can see has tick boxes under a Success criteria heading. So every section made from GLC2O has one. There is nothing to turn on.

- **Where students find it.** The page is first in Key Links, and it is pinned in the sidebar.
- **What it counts.** It counts the boxes a student has ticked under each page's success criteria. The counts are grouped by the class that first linked each page. Each task page is linked from three or four class pages.
- **Who sees the ticks.** Only the student. The ticks are kept in the student's own browser, and nothing reaches you. On another computer the boxes start empty.
- **A new task page.** The page named `_DUPLICATE ME` in Tasks has the same part, so a copy of it starts with success criteria. Keep the heading's words. Settle the words of each box before students start to tick, because a box with new words starts empty again.
- **If you do not want the page.** No switch turns it off. Take the boxes under the Success criteria headings out of the task pages.

{{picture:subject-guidance-and-careers-2}}
alt: The My Progress page on the website of a section made from GLC2O, with a count of ticked boxes for each task page.

[Let students track their progress](page:my-progress) says how to write success criteria on a page of your own. [Checklists students tick](page:task-boxes) says how the boxes work.

The pages also show these:

- **A question students check themselves.** The page Money In, Money Out, in Concepts, has one. It gives the gross pay and the deductions on a pay stub, and asks which of four amounts belongs on a budget's income line. A student picks one and presses {{control:quizCheck}}, or presses {{control:quizShowAnswer}}. An explanation named Why appears after the student answers. Nothing a student answers reaches you. See [Questions students check themselves](page:quizzes).
- **A checklist students tick.** Every class page has one, under the heading Things to do before our next class. These boxes are not counted on {{control:myProgressTitle}}, because their heading is not a success criteria heading. See [Checklists students tick](page:task-boxes).
- **Amounts of money.** The page The Cost of a Month, in Activities, writes its amounts with `\$`, which is how to write a dollar sign that means money. See [Mathematics and chemistry notation](page:notation).
- **Pages written once and linked from class pages.** The activities and the tasks are each written once. Class pages link to them. The Budget is linked from four class pages in the third unit. See [One lesson, written once](page:one-lesson).
- **Diagrams drawn from text.** The page The Pathway Plan, in Tasks, has one. So does How You Actually Decide, in Concepts. See [Diagrams drawn from text](page:diagrams-from-text).
- **Tables and boxes.** The page What This Site Can Do, in Style, shows each with examples from the course. See [Tables, boxes and footnotes](page:tables-boxes-footnotes).

- **Curriculum expectations and a map of them.** The Curriculum folder has a page for every expectation. Lesson pages show the expectations they address, under the heading Curriculum connection. See [Curriculum expectations and the coverage map](page:coverage).

## Folders made for the subject

- **Activities** has six pages. They are Reading a Job Ad, Researching a Pathway, The Cost of a Month, The Informational Interview, The Mock Application and The Skills Inventory.
- **Tasks** has seven: The Application, The Budget, The Decision, The Pathway Plan, The Plan Defence, The Skills Inventory Task and The Work Investigation. It is the folder whose work counts for marks.
- **Style** holds How This Site Is Organised and What This Site Can Do.

[The folders Plantoir treats specially](page:special-folders) says what the folder for marks does.

## What GLC2O does not use

GLC2O shows no picture and no video, and has no page that prints as a handout. A page can hold a picture or print as a handout all the same. See [Pictures, PDFs and the Media folder](page:media) and [A handout that prints](page:printing).
