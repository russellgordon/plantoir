## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Tech Headlines]] — an embedded device in the news, and
   whether the failure was hardware, software, or the seam between them
2. Bench time: [[The Embedded Device]] working end to end — the weakest
   row you named last class first — sensor in, actuator out, and a fault
   log that shows the chase rather than the conclusion
3. When hardware and software disagree, halve the system and test the
   halves: [[Debugging Hardware and Software Together]]
4. Demonstrations in rotation. Every device leaves with two written
   comments — one from me, one from the bench that watched — and next
   class is where you act on them
5. Put today's journal entry beside your first one: [[Showing Growth]]
