Everything we do in this course points at one or more of these expectations,
from **ENG2D — English, Grade 10, Academic**. Lesson pages link here so you can always
see *why* we are doing something.

> [!info] Where these come from
> The wording below is the Ontario curriculum's own. See
> [[About These Expectations]] — including a note about the codes.

The four strands are taught together rather than in turn: you read to
write, write about what you read, and talk about all of it.
