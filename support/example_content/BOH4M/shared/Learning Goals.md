What you should be able to do by the end of BOH4M, in plain words first
and in the Ministry's words underneath.

## By the end of the course you should be able to

- **Say how an organization is put together**, and why it is arranged
  that way rather than another way.
- **Diagnose a people problem** — motivation, conflict, communication,
  workload — and tell the symptom from the cause.
- **Lead a small piece of work** with other people, including when the
  team disagrees.
- **Analyse a strategy** with the standard tools, and say what would have
  to be true for it to work.
- **Plan a change** that accounts for resistance, for the law, and for
  how you would know it was working.
- **Write and speak like a manager**: recommendation first, evidence
  attached, cost named.

## The self-check that matters

Hand someone a real organization with a real problem. Can you say what is
wrong, what you would do, what it costs, and how you would know in three
months? That is the whole course, and it is exactly what
[[The Case Examination]] asks.
