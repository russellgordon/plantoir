1. **Ask the person beside you.** Half of English is talking about a text
   with somebody who read it differently.
2. **Read the page**: [[Close Reading]], [[The Extended Essay]],
   [[Research and Sources]], [[Getting Unstuck]].
3. **Ask me during the work part of the period.**
4. **Come to [[Help Sessions]]** — Tuesdays and Thursdays at lunch.
5. **Email.** Slowest, still works.

## Four openings that work

- "I have a thesis and no evidence."
- "I have evidence and no thesis."
- "My essay is organised by the text instead of by my argument."
- "I read the chapter three times and I have nothing."

The third is the most common problem in Grade 11 essays and takes about
ten minutes to fix at a table with the outline in front of us.
