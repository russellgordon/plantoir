A checklist you write on a page becomes a list of boxes that each student can tick on the class website. This page says how to write one, and where a student's ticks are kept.

## Write the list

1. In Obsidian, type each item on a line of its own. Start the line with a hyphen, then square brackets with a space between them:

```text wrap
- [ ] Bring a pencil
- [ ] Finish the practice questions
- [ ] Read the next page
```

2. Preview the section and open the page. Each item has a box, and clicking a box ticks it.

Nothing else is needed. Every checklist box on the class website can be ticked, on every page. The one exception is a box inside a question students check themselves, where the boxes are the question's choices: see [Questions students check themselves](page:quizzes).

The example course's class pages use a checklist under the heading Things to do before our next class.

## Where the ticks are kept

- **In the student's own browser.** A tick stays when the student reloads the page, and when they move to another page and come back.
- **Nowhere else.** Nothing is sent to you or to anyone. You cannot see which boxes a student has ticked.
- **Your page never changes.** A student's tick is not written into your course.

## What a student may notice

- **Another computer or another browser starts without the student's ticks.** The ticks belong to one browser on one website.
- **A changed item loses its tick.** If you change the first line of an item, its box goes back to how you wrote it, for every student.
- **A renamed or moved page loses all its ticks.** If you rename the page or move it to another folder, every box on it goes back to how you wrote it.

A box you wrote as `- [ ]` is empty. A box you wrote as `- [x]` is ticked until a student unticks it.

For this reason, settle a checklist's wording before students start to use it.

## On paper

When a student prints the page, the boxes print as that student ticked them.

## Count the ticks

Boxes under a page's success criteria can be counted for each student on a page named My Progress. See [Let students track their progress](page:my-progress).
