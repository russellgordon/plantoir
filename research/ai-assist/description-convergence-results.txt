#114: one description per tool - what each router text is worth on the
smaller assistant. Measured 2026-09-26, before the decision landed, as the
mac's reference for the DIRECTION. It is not the Windows measurement the
decision requires (that one is owed on Windows' own hardware, before and
after, with the veto in doc 10 -> "One description per tool").

CONDITIONS
  Mac16,8 (M4 Pro), 48 GiB, macOS 26.6. llama.cpp b10435, Metal.
  Weights: qwen2.5-1.5b-instruct-q4_k_m.gguf (the SMALLER assistant) ONLY.
  The larger assistant (Qwen3-4B) was ABSENT and was not downloaded.
  Flags: AssistServerHost.serverArguments, small tier; temperature 0; the
  app's request body; the shipped system prompt with the date appended.

ARMS (built by description-arms.py beside this file; the builder was checked
byte-identical to the arms the numbers below were taken with)
  A  the contract's text - toolSchemas.local, which since #114 is pinned in
     assist-cases.json -> toolDescriptions. The convergence target.
  B  Windows' router text today: Briefly() of PlantoirTools.cs' descriptions.
     Differs from A in exactly five tools: check_section, publish_pages,
     unpublish_pages, read_remembered_timetable, add_next_class.
  C  Briefly() of the contract text: what Windows would read if it kept
     trimming. Differs from A in two: publish_pages, add_next_class.
  Everything but the descriptions of those tools is identical across arms.

READING
  On the smaller tier, on this hardware, the contract's text is never worse
  than Windows' current router text and is better where the suites disagree:
  one 10/10 cut-off runaway fewer on the 29-probe suite, +30 control trials on
  teachers-say. No arm inverts polarity. Trimming the contract text with
  Briefly() (C) is NOT free: level on the 29-probe suite, 40 control trials
  worse than A on teachers-say - which is why the decision is to stop
  trimming rather than to trim the new text. The two timetable probes swap
  between A and B and net to zero, which is why doc 10's veto is written per
  TOOL rather than per probe.
