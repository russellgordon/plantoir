What you should be able to do by the end of THJ2O, in plain words first
and in the Ministry's words underneath.

## By the end of the course you should be able to

- **Identify** the plants on our sites, and say what each needs.
- **Explain how a plant works** well enough to diagnose why one is
  failing.
- **Propagate and grow** plants that survive, and keep the record that
  proves it.
- **Work safely** with hand and power tools, and know your rights on a
  job.
- **Measure, calculate, and build** something that lasts outdoors, from
  a drawing.
- **Judge a practice** by what it does to the site, the water, and the
  people nearby.
- **Show what you can do** to someone who might hire you.

## The self-check that matters

Two things. Can you take a bare patch of ground, plan what should happen
to it, and carry that out safely with the right tools? And can you hand
the next person a record they could actually follow? That is
[[The Site Project]], and it is the course.
