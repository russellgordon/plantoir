Everything marked, with the criteria published before you start.

- [[The Story Behind the Story]] — Unit 1. Write a short story, then
  write about the choices you made in it.
- [[The Scene, Staged]] — Unit 2. Direct and perform one scene of
  *Romeo and Juliet*, with a director's note that defends every choice.
- [[The Literary Essay]] — Unit 2. One argument about the play, proved
  with three passages and complicated once.
- [[The Seminar]] — Unit 3. Lead a discussion of
  *Moon of the Crusted Snow* that other people want to be in.
- [[The Media Deconstruction]] — Unit 4. Take a media text apart, then
  make two linked texts for different audiences.
- [[Final Examination]] — the final evaluation, in the examination
  period.

> [!tip] Read the criteria table twice
> Once before you start, once when you think you are finished. Most of
> the distance between a level 3 and a level 4 in this room is a
> criterion somebody read only once.
