> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class. **Groups of three.** The evidence for quantised
> energy levels, seen directly.

Gas discharge tubes through a diffraction grating: hydrogen, helium, neon,
mercury. Each shows a set of sharp lines, not a continuous rainbow, and
each set is different.

## Safety

Discharge tubes run at high voltage and get hot. I switch them; you look.
Do not touch the tube or the supply, and report a cracked tube rather than
handling it.

## Method

1. View each tube through the grating and sketch the line positions
   against a scale.
2. Measure the angle to at least three lines for hydrogen and compute
   their wavelengths with $n\lambda = d\sin\theta$.
3. Compare with the accepted Balmer wavelengths.
4. View an incandescent bulb for contrast: a continuous spectrum.

## Analysis

1. Why lines rather than a continuum? Answer in terms of energy levels
   and $E = hf$.
2. Compute the photon energy for one of your hydrogen lines, in joules
   and in electronvolts, to a defensible number of figures — see
   [[Units and Significant Figures]].
3. Astronomers identify the elements in a star they will never visit.
   Explain how, using your own data as the argument.
