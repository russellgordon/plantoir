One page per class, newest at the top of your teacher's list: the agenda
we ran and anything to file before next time. Away? Start here, run the
warm-up yourself, and make the thing before reading anything about it —
the page holds the plan, but in this newsroom the media gets made before
the principle gets its name.

Our semester runs in four units:

- **Unit 1 — The Newsroom Opens**: news sense, ledes, and interviewing, ending in [[Your First Byline]].
- **Unit 2 — The Athletics Beat**: photos, video, and clean audio from a real event, ending in [[The Athletics Package]].
- **Unit 3 — The Shop**: measuring, safety, and the design process in material, in [[The Sign Shop]] and [[The Rig]].
- **Unit 4 — Publication**: design, typography, and platforms — [[The Front Page]], shown at [[Publication Day]].
