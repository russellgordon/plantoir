## What was read for the two new moderates

- **GHSA-3g3m-2fjr-6pw4**, glob 9.0.0 to 14.0.1: "AbortSignal cancellation
  bypass allows post-timeout filesystem traversal". Quartz has glob 11.0.0
  only under rimraf 6.0.1. Quartz's one rimraf call is
  `rimraf(path.join(output, "*"), { glob: true, maxRetries: 5, retryDelay: 50 })`
  in `quartz/build.ts`, with no `signal`; rimraf's `opt-arg.js` passes a signal
  to glob only when its caller gave one. Nothing to cancel, so not reached.
  Accepted on the existing `quartz` / `glob` entry.
- **GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249**, minimatch < 11.0.1: "potential denial of service
  via globparts amplification". minimatch 10.0.1 (Quartz's `ignorePatterns`
  in `build.ts`) and 3.1.2 (serve-handler's own configuration). The patterns
  are Quartz's and serve-handler's, never a teacher's notes or a student's
  request, which is the existing entry's reason. Accepted on the `quartz` /
  `minimatch` entry.
