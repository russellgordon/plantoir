Before the final submission:

- [ ] [[First Entry]] present
- [ ] At least four investigation reflections
- [ ] At least one entry about work that went badly
- [ ] A [[Showing Growth]] comparison entry
- [ ] Three pieces of work you chose, each with a sentence on why
- [ ] [[Culminating Reflection]] completed
- [ ] Everything readable — headings, dates, links that work

## How it is assessed

| Criterion | What I look for |
| --- | --- |
| Selection | The pieces chosen show a range, not just the best marks |
| Reflection | Specific, honest, about thinking rather than feelings |
| Growth | Change is shown with evidence, not asserted |
| Made for a reader | Headings, dates and links that work, so a stranger can find their way through it without you beside them |
