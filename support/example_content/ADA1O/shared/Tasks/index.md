Every performance task in this course is rehearsed in class, and that is
deliberate: the working periods are where I watch you make the thing and
where we talk about it, and two of the three kinds of evidence behind your
mark exist nowhere else. [[How Marks Work]] explains how the pieces fit
together, and every success criteria table below traces back to our
[[Learning Goals]].

Every task here is performed with other people, and every one of them is
marked individually — each page names the part of it that is yours.

## The seven tasks

- [[Tableau Story Sequence]] — your group tells a complete story in five
  frozen pictures.
- [[Improvisation Showcase]] — you and a partner build a scene from nothing
  but three slips of paper.
- [[Scene Study from a Story]] — a fairy tale, myth, or legend becomes a
  scripted scene shaped by one convention.
- [[Drama in the World]] — a form or tradition, researched properly, taught
  to us by putting us on our feet in it.
- [[Production Roles Interview]] — you become a theatre professional and
  take questions in role.
- [[Culminating Performance]] — everything the course built, on stage for a
  real audience.
- [[Milestone Journal Entries]] — four times this semester, in class, you
  look back at what you just made and say what it taught you.

Underneath all of them, your [[Drama Journal]] runs all course long — it
lives in Portfolios, because that is where your growth gets recorded rather
than performed. Most of it is practice and is not marked; the four entries
that are marked have their own page above.
