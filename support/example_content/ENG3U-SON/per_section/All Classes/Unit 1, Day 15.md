## Agenda

1. Task launch: [[Two Stories, One Thread]] — your first essay of the
   course. Success criteria shared and explained before you write a word.
2. Choose your pairing: two of "Boys and Girls," "The Lottery," "Borders,"
   "Happy Endings," and start looking for the thread that actually connects
   them
3. Workshop: [[Integrating Evidence - The Quote Sandwich|Integrating Evidence: The Quote Sandwich]] — you'll need it for the evidence you gather over the next few days
4. Start your thesis, using [[Building a Thesis Statement]], and sketch a
   rough outline — just the order you think your evidence will go in

## Things to do before our next class

      — next class I circulate and give feedback on both before you draft
