This page lists what a Science website made with Plantoir can show. Each item names a page of a ready-made Science course where you can see it, and the Help page that says how it is done.

## The ready-made Science courses

Eight codes have ready-made pages:

- **SNC1W** and **SNC2D**, Science, Grades 9 and 10.
- **SBI3U** and **SBI4U**, Biology, Grades 11 and 12.
- **SCH3U** and **SCH4U**, Chemistry, Grades 11 and 12.
- **SPH3U** and **SPH4U**, Physics, Grades 11 and 12.

To get one, choose {{menu:newCourse}}, type its code and leave {{control:newCourseSheetPrePopulateWithExampleContent}} turned on. [Make a new course](page:new-course) has the steps. Each comes with its folders, its shared pages, and class pages for every section, dated across the semester. All but the last class page are published, and the last is held back as an example of a page students do not see yet. So the first deploy may ask about classes dated later. See [Classes dated after the next class, at Deploy](page:later-classes).

To read one without teaching from it, put it on the Reference Courses shelf. See [Bring in last year's courses](page:import-for-reference).

Any other Science code offers a starting point for its subject instead: science, biology, chemistry, physics, earth and space science, or environmental science.

## What the pages show

- **Chemical equations and formulas, typeset.** SCH3U's page Combustion, in Concepts, sets out the equations for complete and incomplete combustion, with subscripts, states and reaction arrows. The class page for Unit 2, Day 8 links to it. Every one of the eight but SPH4U writes chemistry this way somewhere. See [Mathematics and chemistry notation](page:notation).

{{picture:subject-science-1}}
alt: The page Combustion on the class website, with its chemical equations typeset.

- **Mathematics, typeset.** All eight write their mathematics between dollar signs. SPH3U's page Projectile Motion, in Concepts, has a table that sets the horizontal and vertical motion side by side. The class page for Unit 1, Day 12 links to it. See [Mathematics and chemistry notation](page:notation).
- **Diagrams drawn from text.** SPH3U's page The Doppler Effect, in Concepts, has one. So does SCH3U's page Sorting Five Reactions, in Investigations. See [Diagrams drawn from text](page:diagrams-from-text).

{{picture:subject-science-2}}
alt: The page The Doppler Effect on the class website, with its diagram.

- **Reference pages students come back to.** The six Grade 11 and 12 sciences have a Reference folder. In SCH3U it holds Solubility Rules, Polyatomic Ions, The Activity Series, Naming Rules at a Glance, and Significant Figures and Units. Class pages link to them: Unit 4, Day 1 of SCH3U sends students to Solubility Rules to check a grid of predictions. See [One lesson, written once](page:one-lesson).

{{picture:subject-science-3}}
alt: The page Solubility Rules on the class website, with its table of rules.

- **Investigations written once and linked from class pages.** Each of the eight has an Investigations folder. SNC1W's holds Flame Tests and Building Series and Parallel Circuits, among others.
- **A checklist students tick.** Every class page has one, under the heading Things to do before our next class. See [Checklists students tick](page:task-boxes).
- **A program shown on a page.** SNC1W's page Getting Started with Python, in Tutorials, shows short programs for students to read. See [Show code on a page](page:code-blocks).
- **Tables, boxes and footnotes.** The page What This Site Can Do, in Style, shows each with examples from the course. See [Tables, boxes and footnotes](page:tables-boxes-footnotes).

- **Curriculum expectations and a map of them.** The Curriculum folder has a page for every expectation. Lesson pages show the expectations they address, under the heading Curriculum connection. See [Curriculum expectations and the coverage map](page:coverage).

## Simulations

A Science page can hold a PhET simulation, such as Gas Properties or Projectile Motion. Students open it in the page and use it. The class website carries its own copy, and a student's browser asks nothing of PhET.

None of the eight ready-made courses has a simulation on a page yet. You add one with a short block that names it. See [Science simulations](page:phet).

## Folders made for the subject

- **Investigations** holds the labs and inquiries.
- **Reference**, in the six Grade 11 and 12 sciences, holds the pages students look things up in.
- **Concepts**, **Discussions**, **Exercises**, **Portfolios** and **Tutorials** hold what their names say.
- **Style** holds the page What This Site Can Do.
- **Tasks** is the folder whose work counts for marks.

[The folders Plantoir treats specially](page:special-folders) says what the folder for marks does.

## What the Science courses do not use

None of the eight has a question that students check themselves, a page that prints as a handout, a picture or a video. A page can hold each all the same. See [Questions students check themselves](page:quizzes), [A handout that prints](page:printing), [Pictures, PDFs and the Media folder](page:media) and [Put a video on a page](page:videos).
