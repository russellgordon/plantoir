Everything for this course is here — what we did each day, the ideas,
the activities, and every piece of work you are marked on. The course is
ten weeks long, so five minutes now is worth more than it would be in a
full-year course.

## Where things are

- **All Classes** — one page per period. What we did, and what to do
  before next time. If you were away, start here and work backwards.
- **Concepts** — the ideas, explained. Read these after the class that
  met the idea, not before.
- **Activities** — the things we do in class: inventories, interviews,
  job ads, budgets. Each page has the method, so you can run it again on
  your own.
- **Portfolios** — the three entries that build the folder you present
  at the end.
- **Tasks** — everything you are marked on, with what earns the marks.
- **Tutorials** — how to do the things this course asks for.
- **Setup** — how the class runs, what to bring, how marks work.
- **Style** — how this site works, including what it can do.

## Three things worth knowing

**Search is the fastest way in.** Press `Ctrl`+`K` or `Cmd`+`K` and
type. It searches inside the pages, not only the titles, so searching
for "OSAP" or "résumé" finds every page that mentions it.

**Links go both ways.** At the foot of most pages is a list of pages
that link to it. Land on [[Skills That Transfer]] and that list shows
you every activity and task where the idea is used.

**Small arrows hide things.** A line with an arrow on the end opens when
you click it — usually a question with an answer underneath. Try
answering before you open it; reading an answer feels like learning and
is not.

> [!note] The checkboxes here are yours
> Several pages have lists that look like `- [ ]`. Tick them as you work
> through them: your browser keeps the ticks on this computer, nothing is
> sent anywhere, and the site has no idea who you are. On another computer
> they start again, so copy a list you need everywhere into your own
> notebook or document.

## If a page is missing

A class page appears on the day we reach it. If a link on today's page
does not work yet, that is because that class has not happened. Ask, and
I will tell you whether it is deliberate or an oversight.
